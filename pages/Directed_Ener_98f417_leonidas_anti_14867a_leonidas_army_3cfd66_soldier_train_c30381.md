## Introduction

Leonidas did not become a serious Army capability simply because it could disable drones. It gained momentum because soldiers, not company engineers, began operating it. The transition from contractor-led demonstrations to Army-run training and developmental testing answered a question that every military technology eventually faces: can ordinary units use, maintain and evaluate the system under military conditions? For the Army’s Indirect Fire Protection Capability–High Power Microwave (IFPC-HPM) effort, New Equipment Training (NET) and [Engineering]({{ 'engineering/' | relative_url }}) Developmental Testing (EDT) became the bridge between a promising directed-energy prototype and equipment that could plausibly enter operational service.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-systems-to-u-s-army-completes-new-equipment-training-and-engineering-developmental-testing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to...May 15, 2024 — ‍Los Angeles, California – May 15, 2024 – Epirus announced...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2024</span></span></span>

## What New Equipment Training Tested

In Army acquisition, New Equipment Training is more than a familiarisation course. It is the process through which soldiers learn whether a new system can realistically fit into military operations. For Leonidas, this meant moving beyond demonstrations where contractor personnel controlled setup, operation and troubleshooting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-systems-to-u-s-army-completes-new-equipment-training-and-engineering-developmental-testing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to...May 15, 2024 — ‍Los Angeles, California – May 15, 2024 – Epirus announced...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2024</span></span></span>

The training effort examined practical questions:

* How quickly can soldiers learn system operation?
* Can crews conduct routine [maintenance]({{ 'maintenance/' | relative_url }}) without contractor intervention?

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Are controls and software understandable to military operators?
* Can air-defence personnel integrate the system into existing defensive procedures?
* What level of technical support is required to keep the system available for missions?

</div>

The Army Air Defense Artillery community participated in the training process, giving the programme feedback from the branch that would eventually employ the capability. According to programme announcements, soldiers were trained on system use while operational capability and limitation assessments were also conducted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/andylowery_epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-activity-7196498881017188352-oKq2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: linkedin.com">[LinkedIn]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">linkedin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Linked In Andy Lowery&#x27;s Post Epirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Linked In Andy Lowery&#x27;s Post Epirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because military adoption is often limited by usability rather than performance. A weapon that works under ideal conditions may still fail if it requires specialised expertise, excessive maintenance or continuous contractor support.

## Why Developmental Testing Changes the Question

Contractor demonstrations generally ask whether a technology can achieve a desired effect. Developmental testing asks whether the military can depend on it.

The first IFPC-HPM prototype delivered in November 2023 arrived after government acceptance testing that validated important safety characteristics and allowed the Army to begin handling the system directly. Army evaluation activities then shifted toward understanding how the capability performed under military oversight rather than company-controlled events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-delivers-ifpc-hpm-counter-swarm-system-to-u-s-army-developing-pathway-to-field-high-power-microwave-capability" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Acuity Partners]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to U.S. Army, Completes New Equipment Training and Engineering Developmental Testing...</span></span></span>

Engineering Developmental Testing examined factors that rarely attract public attention but often determine programme success:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Reliability over repeated use.
* Maintainability in field conditions.
* Safety for personnel and nearby equipment.
* Integration with Army air-defence concepts.
* Performance consistency across different operational scenarios.

</div>

The Army's testing process was designed not only to verify [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) effectiveness but also to generate the data needed for future acquisition decisions. Defence reporting noted that Army evaluations would feed the development of tactics, techniques and procedures for operational employment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defensenews.com/battlefield-tech/2023/11/01/army-gets-first-high-power-microwave-prototype-to-counter-drone-swarms/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defensenews.com">[Defense News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defensenews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Army to counter drone swarms as part of the service&#x27;s Indirect Fire Protection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defense NewsArmy gets first high-power microwave prototype to counter ...November 1, 2023 — 1 Nov 2023 — Epirus has delivered the first p...</span></span></span>

That represents a fundamental change in status. Once a system begins informing doctrine, training procedures and operational concepts, it is no longer being treated merely as an experiment.

## What Operators Can Reveal That Demos Cannot

Soldiers evaluate equipment differently from developers.

A contractor demonstration is typically designed to showcase strengths. Military operators focus on friction points: setup time, workload, maintenance burden, reliability and ease of use during realistic missions. As soldiers began working with IFPC-HPM systems, they generated a form of evidence that laboratory demonstrations could not provide.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://breakingdefense.com/2023/11/army-gets-its-hands-on-new-high-power-microwave-prototype-from-epirus/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: breakingdefense.com">[Breaking Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">breakingdefense.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">army gets its hands on new high power microwave prototype from epirus</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Breaking DefenseArmy gets its hands on new high-power microwave...1 Nov 2023 — The US Army has accepted delivery of Epirus&#x27;s first high...</span></span></span>

Operator feedback can reveal issues such as:

**Crew workload.** A technically successful weapon may still be impractical if it requires excessive attention from operators already managing multiple threats.

**Training burden.** Systems requiring lengthy qualification pipelines face greater barriers to fielding.

**Maintenance realities.** Soldiers identify recurring faults, replacement requirements and sustainment challenges that rarely appear in demonstration events.

**Operational fit.** Units assess whether a capability complements existing air-defence procedures or creates new complications.

Evidence from subsequent Army discussions suggests that developmental testing produced both positive performance data and insights into areas needing improvement. Later programme announcements specifically referenced demonstrated effectiveness while also highlighting efforts to make the systems easier for soldiers to employ.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.microwavejournal.com/articles/42858-epirus-secures-17-m-contract-from-us-army" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: microwavejournal.com">[Microwave Journal+2PR Newswire]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">microwavejournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Microwave Journal Epirus Secures $17 M Contract From U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ArmyOct 15, 2024 — In May 2024, Epirus announced the delivery of four IFPC-HPM systems to the Army after successful completion of enginee...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

That combination is typical of successful military prototyping. The goal is not merely to prove that a weapon works, but to identify what must change before widespread operational use becomes practical.

## The Shift From Prototype to Operational Candidate

The significance of NET and EDT lies in what they enabled next. Completion of training and developmental testing positioned the Army to consider issuing the systems to operational units and conducting broader evaluations with military personnel.
