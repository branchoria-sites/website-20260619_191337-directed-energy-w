## Introduction

A high-power microwave pulse does not affect every drone in a swarm in the same way, even when all of them appear to be exposed to the same burst. One drone may fall from the sky immediately, another may reboot and recover, a third may lose its navigation system but continue flying, while a fourth may show little visible effect at all. This variation is not necessarily a sign that the weapon failed. It is a consequence of how microwave energy interacts with electronics, antennas, wiring, software, and physical orientation. Studies of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) susceptibility to strong electromagnetic fields consistently show that the outcome depends on which subsystem absorbs energy, how much energy reaches it, and how the drone is designed to respond when something goes wrong. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/2/393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strong Electromagnetic Interference and Protection in UAVsby Z Zhang · 2024 · Cited by 65 — This paper aims to introduce the potentia...</span></span></span>

Understanding these different failure modes is important for evaluating microwave-based swarm defence. A swarm that appears partly unaffected after a pulse may still have suffered widespread degradation, just not in a uniform or immediately visible way.

## Electronics That Microwave Energy Can Disrupt

Microwave weapons are generally aimed at electronics rather than airframes. The pulse can induce unwanted voltages and currents inside a drone through antennas, cables, circuit traces, and other conductive paths. Research on UAV electromagnetic vulnerability identifies several particularly sensitive subsystems. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/2/393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strong Electromagnetic Interference and Protection in UAVsby Z Zhang · 2024 · Cited by 65 — This paper aims to introduce the potentia...</span></span></span>

These include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* **Flight-control electronics**, which stabilise and command the aircraft.
* **Navigation systems**, including GPS receivers and associated sensors.
* **Data links**, which connect the drone to an operator or network.
* **Power-management circuits**, which distribute electrical power.
* **Electronic speed controllers**, which regulate motor operation. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/2/393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strong Electromagnetic Interference and Protection in UAVsby Z Zhang · 2024 · Cited by 65 — This paper aims to introduce the potentia...</span></span></span>

</div>

The specific subsystem affected often determines the visible result. A disrupted radio link may trigger a return-to-home routine. A damaged GPS receiver may leave the drone flying but unable to navigate accurately. A flight-controller malfunction may cause immediate instability and loss of control. Damage to power electronics can produce a complete shutdown. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/14/21/4332" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Investigation on Electromagnetic Immunity of Unmanned...by R Kubacki · 2025 · Cited by 2 — Microwave field incidents on a drone can...</span></span></span>

This means that two drones receiving similar electromagnetic exposure can display very different behaviour simply because the energy couples into different components.

## Why Identical Exposure Can Produce Different Outcomes

The phrase “identical exposure” is misleading. Even when drones occupy the same microwave beam, they rarely experience exactly the same electromagnetic conditions.

### Orientation Changes What Receives Energy

Microwave energy enters a drone through [coupling paths]({{ 'coupling-paths/' | relative_url }}) such as antennas and wiring. The effectiveness of that coupling depends strongly on orientation. A drone banking, climbing, or turning may present antennas, cables, or circuit assemblies to the incoming field in a different way than a neighbouring aircraft. Electromagnetic studies show that antenna orientation alone can significantly alter received signal strength and energy transfer. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.01442" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Impact of 3D UWB Antenna Radiation Pattern on Air-to-Ground Drone ConnectivityOctober 2, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2018</span></span></span>

As a result, one drone may absorb enough energy to disrupt critical systems while another, only metres away, receives a much smaller effective dose.

### Internal Design Differences Matter

Swarm members are not always electronically identical. Even drones built from the same commercial design may contain different component revisions, firmware versions, repair histories, or manufacturing tolerances.

Electromagnetic immunity research shows that susceptibility depends on detailed factors including circuit layout, shielding quality, grounding practices, and protective components. Small design differences can alter how induced currents travel through the vehicle. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/2/393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strong Electromagnetic Interference and Protection in UAVsby Z Zhang · 2024 · Cited by 65 — This paper aims to introduce the potentia...</span></span></span>

This creates a distribution of vulnerabilities rather than a single threshold. Instead of every drone failing at the same field strength, some fail earlier while others remain functional.

### Frequency and Polarisation Effects

The effectiveness of microwave coupling depends not only on power but also on frequency, polarisation, modulation, and pulse characteristics. Research into UAV electromagnetic compatibility has found that these parameters influence which systems are affected and how severe the effects become. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202509.2161/v1/download" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: preprints.org">[Preprints]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">preprints.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The strong microwaves are investigated...Read more...</span></span></span>

A pulse that strongly couples into a particular communications antenna may have a weaker effect on another subsystem. Consequently, drones with different antenna layouts or electronic architectures can respond differently within the same engagement.

## Why Failure Often Appears Random

From the ground, microwave effects can look unpredictable. In reality, many apparently random outcomes follow understandable [engineering]({{ 'engineering/' | relative_url }}) pathways.

A drone may:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* Lose its command link and enter an autonomous mode.
* Suffer GPS degradation while maintaining basic flight.
* Reboot its flight computer and temporarily stop responding.
* Experience corrupted sensor data.
* Lose motor control and crash.
* Sustain permanent electronic damage. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/14/21/4332" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Investigation on Electromagnetic Immunity of Unmanned...by R Kubacki · 2025 · Cited by 2 — Microwave field incidents on a drone can...</span></span></span>

</div>

The visible behaviour depends not only on what was disrupted but also on the drone's software. Modern flight-control systems often include fail-safe routines designed to cope with faults. One aircraft may land automatically after losing navigation, while another may hover, drift, or attempt to return to its launch point. The pulse may therefore expose differences in software resilience as much as differences in hardware vulnerability. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/2/393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strong Electromagnetic Interference and Protection in UAVsby Z Zhang · 2024 · Cited by 65 — This paper aims to introduce the potentia...</span></span></span>

This is one reason microwave engagements can produce mixed results within a swarm even when every aircraft is affected to some degree.

## Shielding Does Not Create Equal Protection

A common assumption is that shielding either works or does not. In practice, shielding effectiveness varies widely.

Research on UAV protection against strong electromagnetic interference notes that antennas, wiring harnesses, cable penetrations, and sensor openings often become pathways through which energy reaches sensitive electronics. Even when a component is enclosed, attached conductors can carry unwanted energy inside. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/2/393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI+2radioeng.cz]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strong Electromagnetic Interference and Protection in UAVsby Z Zhang · 2024 · Cited by 65 — This paper aims to introduce the potentia...</span></span></span>

Experimental work examining microwave effects on UAVs has identified cables and communication paths as important coupling routes. This means two drones with apparently similar protective measures may experience very different internal stress levels. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/362260176_Investigation_on_Falling_and_Damage_Mechanisms_of_UAV_Illuminated_by_HPM_Pulses" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Investigation on Falling and Damage Mechanisms of UAV...13 Oct 2022 — Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV), when illuminated by hig...</span></span></span>

Shielding therefore tends to reduce vulnerability rather than eliminate it. The result is often a spectrum of outcomes rather than a clean division between protected and unprotected aircraft.

## What Failure Variety Means for Swarm Defence

The differing responses of drones inside a single pulse create both advantages and challenges for defenders.

The advantage is that a microwave weapon does not need to achieve identical effects on every target to be useful. A swarm's effectiveness depends on coordination, navigation, communication, and mass.
