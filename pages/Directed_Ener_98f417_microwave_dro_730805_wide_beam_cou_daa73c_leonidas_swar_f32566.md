## Introduction

Leonidas has become one of the most frequently cited examples of how a high-power microwave (HPM) weapon might defeat drone [swarms]({{ 'swarms/' | relative_url }}) through a single engagement rather than a sequence of one-on-one interceptions. Developed by Epirus, the system is often presented as evidence for the broader claim that microwave weapons can conduct “one-to-many” attacks against groups of drones. The most publicised demonstrations have shown dozens of drones being disabled in rapid succession, including claims that large portions of a swarm were defeated with a single microwave pulse. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tap to unmute.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-Drone...Sep 10, 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49...</span></span></span>

Understanding what the tests actually demonstrate is therefore essential before treating them as proof of unlimited counter-swarm effectiveness.

## What the Reported Swarm Demonstrations Claimed

Leonidas was designed specifically around the challenge of defeating large numbers of small unmanned aircraft. Public descriptions of the system emphasise its ability to operate either in a focused mode against individual targets or in a broader-area mode intended to affect multiple drones simultaneously. Epirus has repeatedly highlighted this flexibility as a core advantage over weapons that engage one target at a time. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

The most widely discussed demonstration occurred at Camp Atterbury in Indiana in 2025. According to Epirus, Leonidas disabled 61 of 61 drones flown during a live-fire event that included several operational scenarios. The company stated that the final scenario involved a 49-drone swarm composed of two representative drone types and that the swarm was defeated with a single burst of electromagnetic interference. Military and government observers from multiple countries reportedly attended the event. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Unmanned Airspace]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tap to unmute.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-Drone...Sep 10, 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49...</span></span></span>

## Why the One-to-Many Claim Matters

The phrase “one-to-many” is central to Leonidas marketing and to the broader military interest in microwave weapons. Conventional air-defence systems often consume one interceptor, [one burst]({{ 'one-burst/' | relative_url }}) of ammunition or one laser engagement against one target. Drone swarms attempt to exploit that arithmetic by presenting many targets at once.

Leonidas is presented as reversing that equation. Rather than assigning a separate engagement to every drone, the weapon projects electromagnetic energy into a volume of space containing multiple aircraft. If several drones occupy that volume simultaneously and their electronics are vulnerable to the pulse, multiple vehicles can be disrupted during the same firing sequence. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

The reported 49-drone demonstration became significant precisely because it appeared to offer a visual illustration of this concept. The claim was not merely that 49 drones were eventually defeated, but that a large swarm could be affected through a single electromagnetic event rather than dozens of separate shots. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tap to unmute.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-Drone...Sep 10, 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49...</span></span></span>

From a military planning perspective, this distinction is critical. If a defender can truly engage a dense swarm as a collective target, the economics and tactics of drone defence change substantially. That possibility explains why Leonidas has attracted attention from the U.S. Army, Marine Corps and other defence organisations pursuing counter-swarm programmes. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia+2DefenseScoop]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

## Why Controlled Live-Fire Tests Have Limits

The public demonstrations are impressive, but they do not automatically answer every operational question.

First, demonstration swarms are controlled events. Organisers know the participating drone types, flight paths and safety requirements in advance. Real combat environments introduce unknown aircraft designs, varying electronics, unpredictable formations and competing electromagnetic activity. Public test results therefore show capability under a defined set of conditions rather than performance against every possible swarm. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tap to unmute.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-Drone...Sep 10, 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49...</span></span></span>

Second, the headline numbers can obscure important details. A claim that dozens of drones were defeated in one event does not necessarily mean every drone received identical effects. Some may have crashed immediately, others may have lost control links, and others may have suffered temporary disruption. Public summaries rarely provide the engineering-level breakdown needed to evaluate exactly how each target failed. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tap to unmute.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-Drone...Sep 10, 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49...</span></span></span>

Third, range, spacing and geometry matter. A dense formation presents a very different problem from a dispersed attack. A microwave weapon's one-to-many advantage depends heavily on multiple targets occupying an area that can be illuminated effectively. Public demonstrations typically showcase conditions that highlight this strength. They reveal what the system can do, but not necessarily how performance changes as drones spread out, vary altitude or approach from multiple directions over larger distances. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

Finally, many technical details remain undisclosed. Defence companies generally do not release complete information about power levels, effective ranges, pulse characteristics, target vulnerabilities or failure thresholds.
