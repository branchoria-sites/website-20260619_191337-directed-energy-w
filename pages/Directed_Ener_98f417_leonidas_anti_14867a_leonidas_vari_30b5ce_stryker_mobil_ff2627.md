## Introduction

Stryker Leonidas exists because the drone threat no longer stays around [fixed bases]({{ 'base-defence/' | relative_url }}). Small unmanned aircraft now accompany military operations across entire battlefields, forcing armies to protect moving formations rather than only static facilities. By mounting the Leonidas high-power microwave (HPM) system on the Stryker armoured vehicle, Epirus and General Dynamics Land Systems sought to turn a [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) capability into a manoeuvre asset that could travel with combat units, respond quickly to emerging threats and provide protection wherever troops operate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

The result was not simply a new vehicle variant. Stryker Leonidas became a demonstration of how directed-energy weapons could be integrated into an existing combat vehicle fleet, giving microwave-based counter-[drone defence]({{ 'drone-defence/' | relative_url }}) the mobility, survivability and [battlefield]({{ 'battle-limits/' | relative_url }}) presence needed for modern operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

## Why Vehicle Mounting Changed the Mission

Earlier Leonidas configurations were designed primarily as ground-based counter-drone systems. Effective though they were, fixed or semi-fixed systems create a gap when military units leave protected areas and move across contested terrain. The growth of cheap reconnaissance drones, loitering munitions and swarm attacks has made that gap increasingly dangerous.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

The Stryker integration addressed this problem by shifting microwave defence from site protection to force protection. Instead of defending a single location, the system could accompany armoured formations, convoy operations and dispersed manoeuvre units. Epirus specifically described the integration as bringing counter-electronics capabilities to the front lines and supporting the manoeuvre force rather than remaining tied to rear-area installations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

This mobility matters because high-power microwaves offer a different defensive logic from missiles or guns. A microwave system can potentially affect multiple drones within its engagement area at once, making it attractive against swarm attacks that could overwhelm conventional interceptors. When that capability moves with troops, commanders gain a defensive layer that is available throughout an operation rather than only around pre-planned defended sites.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/land/army-puts-50m-bet-on-next-gen-leonidas-high-power-microwave-counter-drone-tech" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[The War Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">army puts 50m bet on next gen leonidas high power microwave counter drone tech</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The War ZoneArmy Puts $43M Bet On Next Gen Leonidas High Power...Jul 17, 2025 — It does so by transmitting long-pulse microwave energy a...</span></span></span>

A second motivation was integration into mobile short-range air defence concepts. General Dynamics highlighted Stryker Leonidas as a way to add another layer to manoeuvre short-range air defence, combining vehicle mobility with an electronic defeat mechanism that does not rely on traditional ammunition expenditure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gd.com/Articles/2022/10/04/general-dynamics-business-units-to-participate-in-ausa-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gd.com">[General Dynamics]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">general dynamics business units to participate in ausa 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">General DynamicsGeneral Dynamics Business Units to Participate in AUSA...4 Oct 2022 — Stryker Leonidas: This technology demonstrator add...</span></span></span>

## What the Stryker Integration Reused and Adapted

One reason Stryker Leonidas attracted attention was that it did not require the creation of an entirely new combat vehicle. Instead, developers paired the Leonidas microwave array with one of the US Army's most widely fielded armoured vehicle families.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

This approach reused several advantages already present in the Stryker ecosystem:

* **Established mobility:** The Stryker was already designed to move with combat formations across varied terrain.
* **Existing logistics and support structures:** Armies familiar with Stryker operations would not need a completely separate vehicle infrastructure.
* **Survivability and battlefield presence:** The platform offered protection and operational endurance beyond what a trailer-mounted system could provide.
* **Network integration potential:** The vehicle could operate alongside sensors and other air-defence assets already used by manoeuvre forces.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2General Dynamics]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

At the same time, the microwave weapon itself had to be adapted for vehicle use. Epirus emphasised a modular, software-defined architecture and solid-state design intended to reduce size, weight and [deployment]({{ 'deployment/' | relative_url }}) burdens while increasing manoeuvrability. Those characteristics made vehicle integration more practical than would have been the case for older, bulkier microwave technologies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/electronic-warfare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Leonidas High-Power Microwave: Directed Energy...Leonidas is a solid-state, high-power HPM system, utilizing Gallium Nitrid...</span></span></span>

The speed of the programme was also notable. The companies moved from a teaming agreement in 2021 to a publicly unveiled integrated vehicle in 2022, suggesting that much of the effort focused on adapting an existing microwave capability to an established combat platform rather than creating both from scratch.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

## Why the Stryker Was a Logical Choice

The selection of the Stryker reflected operational considerations as much as engineering ones. The vehicle already occupied an important place in US Army brigade formations, making it a natural test bed for a capability intended to accompany ground forces.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

Using a familiar combat vehicle also allowed developers to demonstrate a broader argument: microwave weapons do not have to remain specialised experimental systems. If they can be mounted on mainstream military platforms, they become easier to imagine as part of future force structures. The Stryker version therefore served as a visible proof-of-concept for integrating directed-energy counter-drone systems into everyday military operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

The vehicle additionally helped showcase one of Leonidas' central selling points—countering multiple electronic threats without consuming traditional interceptors.
