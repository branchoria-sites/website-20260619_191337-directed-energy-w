## Introduction

The Army’s decision to order four Leonidas high-power microwave prototypes in 2023 changed the programme’s credibility because it transformed Leonidas from a promising demonstration into equipment that the service was obligated to evaluate, train on, maintain and integrate into a real military capability. The significance was not simply the $66.1 million contract value. The order created a structured pathway from technology demonstration to operational assessment, giving Leonidas a level of institutional validation that most directed-energy systems never receive. By funding multiple systems rather than a single test article, the Army signalled that it was examining not just whether the technology worked, but whether it could function as a deployable [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) capability. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/u-s-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-tm-directed-energy-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...23 Jan 2023 — Epirus collaborates with the RCCTO to rapidly deliver several prototype...</span></span></span>

## What the 2023 Award Actually Funded

In January 2023, the Army’s Rapid Capabilities and Critical Technologies Office (RCCTO) awarded Epirus a contract worth approximately $66.1 million under the Indirect Fire Protection Capability–High Power Microwave (IFPC-HPM) effort. The contract called for the delivery of four Leonidas-derived prototypes rather than a single experimental unit. The programme was designed to explore a microwave-based defence against drone [swarms]({{ 'swarms/' | relative_url }}) and other small unmanned aircraft threats. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/u-s-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-tm-directed-energy-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Breaking Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...23 Jan 2023 — Epirus collaborates with the RCCTO to rapidly deliver several prototype...</span></span></span>

The contract also contained a more important signal: it explicitly envisioned a transition toward a future programme of record if the prototypes proved successful. In defence acquisition terms, that meant the Army was treating Leonidas as a candidate capability rather than a temporary technology demonstration. The award therefore tested not only microwave effects against drones, but also manufacturing, logistics, supportability and military utility. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/u-s-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-tm-directed-energy-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2PR Newswire]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...23 Jan 2023 — Epirus collaborates with the RCCTO to rapidly deliver several prototype...</span></span></span>

This distinction mattered because directed-energy programmes frequently generate impressive demonstrations without progressing into military procurement pipelines. The Army's prototype order placed Leonidas inside an official acquisition framework where performance would be judged against operational requirements rather than marketing claims. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://taskandpurpose.com/news/army-microwave-weapon-drone-swarms-ifpc-hpm-contract/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: taskandpurpose.com">[Task &amp; Purpose]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">taskandpurpose.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">army microwave weapon drone swarms ifpc hpm contract</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Task &amp; PurposeArmy awards Epirus contract for counter-drone microwave...26 Jan 2023 — The IFPC-HPM system is intended to provide a groun...</span></span></span>

## Why Multiple Systems Mattered More Than One Demo

A single prototype can prove that a technology works under controlled conditions. Four prototypes allow a military organisation to determine whether a capability can actually be fielded.

That difference reshaped perceptions of Leonidas.

With multiple systems available, the Army could:

* Conduct testing at different locations and under different conditions.
* Train soldiers while developmental assessments continued.
* Evaluate reliability across more than one unit.
* Examine sustainment and [maintenance]({{ 'maintenance/' | relative_url }}) requirements.
* Explore how several microwave systems might operate as part of a broader air-defence architecture. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-systems-to-u-s-army-completes-new-equipment-training-and-engineering-developmental-testing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to...15 May 2024 — Epirus announced today that it has delivered the final two o...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

The four-system structure also reduced the risk that positive results could be dismissed as the performance of a specially prepared demonstration article. Defence buyers often distinguish between a prototype built to impress evaluators and a prototype built repeatedly enough to reveal manufacturing, reliability and support challenges. By ordering four systems, the Army forced Leonidas to confront those realities. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/u-s-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-tm-directed-energy-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...23 Jan 2023 — Epirus collaborates with the RCCTO to rapidly deliver several prototype...</span></span></span>

Another credibility boost came from competitive selection. According to reporting on the award, Leonidas emerged from Army evaluations against multiple competing systems. That did not guarantee eventual procurement, but it provided evidence that government evaluators had already compared the technology against alternatives before committing to the prototype phase. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://breakingdefense.com/2023/01/us-army-selects-epirus-leonidas-for-high-power-microwave-initiative/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: breakingdefense.com">[Breaking Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">breakingdefense.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">us army selects epirus leonidas for high power microwave initiative</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Breaking DefenseUS Army selects Epirus&#x27; Leonidas for high-power...23 Jan 2023 — Epirus, a technology company developing directed-energy...</span></span></span>

## The Real Test Began After Contract Award

The strongest evidence that the order changed perceptions came after the contract was signed.

In November 2023, Epirus delivered the first IFPC-HPM prototype to the Army after government acceptance testing. Delivery moved Leonidas from contractor-controlled demonstrations into Army hands, where soldiers and programme officials could begin assessing it as military equipment. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://breakingdefense.com/2023/11/army-gets-its-hands-on-new-high-power-microwave-prototype-from-epirus/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: breakingdefense.com">[Breaking Defense+2DefenseScoop]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">breakingdefense.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Breaking Defense Army gets its hands on new high-power microwave</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Breaking DefenseArmy gets its hands on new high-power microwave...November 1, 2023 — 1 Nov 2023 — The delivery marks the first of four p...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1, 2023</span></span></span>

The larger credibility shift occurred in 2024 when all four systems were delivered and the programme completed New Equipment Training (NET) and [Engineering]({{ 'engineering/' | relative_url }}) Developmental Testing (EDT). Those activities required soldiers to learn operation and maintenance procedures and provided structured opportunities to evaluate effectiveness against unmanned aircraft and drone swarms. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-systems-to-u-s-army-completes-new-equipment-training-and-engineering-developmental-testing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to...15 May 2024 — Epirus announced today that it has delivered the final two o...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

A system may demonstrate impressive technical effects yet struggle with usability, sustainment, training burdens or integration requirements. The completion of deliveries, training and developmental testing indicated that Leonidas had progressed beyond the stage where credibility depended solely on vendor demonstrations. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-systems-to-u-s-army-completes-new-equipment-training-and-engineering-developmental-testing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to...15 May 2024 — Epirus announced today that it has delivered the final two o...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

## Why the Four-Prototype Decision Still Matters

The importance of the four-system award lies less in the number itself than in what the number enabled. One prototype could have demonstrated a microwave effect against drones. Four prototypes enabled a military evaluation process.

That distinction changed Leonidas' credibility because it shifted the central question. Before the award, observers largely asked whether the technology could disable drone swarms. After the award, the Army began asking whether the capability could be fielded, sustained, trained on and eventually incorporated into operational air-defence forces.
