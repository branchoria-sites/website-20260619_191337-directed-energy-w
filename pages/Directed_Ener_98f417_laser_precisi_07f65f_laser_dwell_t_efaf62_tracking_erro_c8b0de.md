## Introduction

A laser can score a direct hit on a drone and still fail to destroy it. The reason is that laser lethality depends not merely on striking the target, but on keeping a highly concentrated beam fixed on a very small vulnerable point for long enough to generate damaging heat. Even slight tracking errors can prevent this from happening. If the [beam jitters]({{ 'beam-jitter/' | relative_url }}), wanders, or slides across the airframe, the same amount of energy is spread over a larger area, lowering the temperature reached at any one location and interrupting the heating process needed for a kill. Studies of high-energy laser engagements consistently identify precise aim-point [maintenance]({{ 'maintenance/' | relative_url }}) as a critical requirement for achieving destructive effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-23-105868.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 105868</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIRECTED ENERGY WEAPONS: DOD Should Focus on...Apr 17, 2023 — The time on target depends on tracking the specific aimpoint on the tar...</span></span></span>

Within the broader problem of laser dwell time, tracking accuracy is often the difference between a successful shoot-down and a harmless scorch mark.

## How Beam Wander Spreads Heat

Laser weapons create damage through concentrated energy density. The centre of the beam carries the highest intensity, and effective heating depends on keeping that concentrated energy focused on the same spot. When tracking errors occur, the beam no longer deposits energy into one location continuously. Instead, the heat is distributed across multiple points on the target.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mdpi.com/2304-6732/12/12/1149" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mdpi.com">[MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mdpi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The spatial distribution of the beam energy governs the strike effectiveness of a high-energy laser system...</span></span></span>

Researchers working on laser [drone-defence]({{ 'drone-defence/' | relative_url }}) systems describe this as an aim-point maintenance problem. If a drone moves and the beam is not continuously corrected, the laser spot wanders over the vehicle's surface. The total [laser power]({{ 'power-costs/' | relative_url }}) may remain unchanged, but the energy is no longer concentrated where it can accumulate most effectively.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/News-Stories/Article/4064895/nps-develops-ai-solution-to-automate-drone-defense-with-high-energy-lasers/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[U.S. Navy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nps develops ai solution to automate drone defense with high energy lasers</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NPS Develops AI Solution to Automate Drone Defense with...12 Feb 2025 — This process of continuously firing the laser beam at one sp...</span></span></span>

The result resembles trying to burn through a sheet of material with a magnifying glass while constantly moving the focal point. Instead of one location becoming progressively hotter, many locations become slightly warmer. Temperatures may never reach the threshold required to melt, fracture, ignite, or disable a component.

Several forms of tracking error contribute to this effect:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Jitter:** rapid, small-amplitude vibrations in the pointing system.
* **Beam wander:** movement of the laser spot caused by platform motion, tracking inaccuracies, or atmospheric disturbances.
* **Lag errors:** delays between target motion and beam correction.
* **Optical misalignment:** shifts within the beam-control system that move the laser away from the intended aim point.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2020226721A1/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patents.google.com">[Google Patents+2DLR Electronic Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patents.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google PatentsBeam director for high-energy laser (hel) weaponA beam director system for a high-energy laser (HEL) weapon includes correc...</span></span></span>

</div>

Each error reduces the effective energy density delivered to the critical location on the target.

## Why Vulnerable Aim Points Are Small

The challenge is made harder because the most valuable aim points on a drone are often tiny.

A drone may continue flying even after substantial heating of non-critical structure. Damaging a broad section of fuselage is usually less effective than concentrating energy on a specific weak spot such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* An optical sensor.
* A motor housing.
* A rotor hub or arm.
* Exposed wiring.
* A battery compartment.
* Flight-control electronics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy WeaponsMay 25, 2023 — Directed energy weapons—such as lasers—use energy fired at the speed o...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 25, 2023</span></span></span>

</div>

These components occupy only a small fraction of the visible target area. A tracking system must therefore do more than keep the beam on the drone. It must keep the beam on the right part of the drone.

This distinction is crucial. A beam that remains somewhere on the airframe may appear to be tracking successfully while still failing to generate a kill. If the laser repeatedly drifts from a battery enclosure to surrounding structure, or from a rotor root to a nearby panel, heat is deposited into less vulnerable material and the engagement becomes less effective. The target survives despite being illuminated.

This is why defence organisations and laser researchers often focus on aim-point maintenance rather than simple target tracking. Tracking the object is not enough; the weapon must continuously track the precise damage location.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-23-105868.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 105868</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIRECTED ENERGY WEAPONS: DOD Should Focus on...Apr 17, 2023 — The time on target depends on tracking the specific aimpoint on the tar...</span></span></span>

## What Stabilisation Systems Must Overcome

Maintaining a steady aim point sounds straightforward until the number of moving elements is considered.

The drone may be manoeuvring, rotating, climbing, or descending. The laser platform may also be moving, especially when mounted on ships, vehicles, or aircraft. Optical assemblies can vibrate. Wind can affect pointing systems. Atmospheric turbulence can cause the beam itself to wander. Every disturbance must be measured and corrected in real time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271457780_Pointingtracking_control_design_for_a_high_energy_laser_system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate+2FOI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pointing/tracking control design for a high energy laser...July 1, 2014 — The device tracks a Gaussian beam due to the beam...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 1, 2014</span></span></span>

Modern laser weapon systems therefore incorporate multiple layers of stabilisation and tracking:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Coarse tracking systems that keep the target within the sensor field of view.
* Fine tracking systems that lock onto a specific aim point.
* Beam stabilisation mechanisms that compensate for vibration and disturbances.
* Optical alignment systems that correct errors along the laser path.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/KR101057303B1/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patents.google.com">[Google Patents]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patents.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google PatentsKR101057303B1 - Laser weapon tracking aiming deviceThe present invention is a laser weapon tracking aiming device 1000 that...</span></span></span>

The [engineering]({{ 'engineering/' | relative_url }}) difficulty lies in performing all these corrections fast enough. A drone can change orientation in fractions of a second. Even a tiny delay between detecting motion and repositioning the beam can shift the hotspot away from the desired location.

Recent research efforts have increasingly explored predictive tracking methods and artificial intelligence to anticipate target motion rather than merely react to it. The goal is to reduce the gap between where the drone is now and where it will be when the beam arrives and heating continues.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/395192454_AI-Based_Predictive_Laser_Beam_Control_for_UAV_Interception_in_Directed_Energy_Weapon_Systems_Using_Velocity-Based_Trajectory_Prediction" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) AI-Based Predictive Laser Beam Control for UAV...August 30, 2025 — 9 Aug 2025 — The predictive model achieves a tracki...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 30, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why Small Errors Can Cause Large Drops in Effectiveness

Tracking performance is not a linear problem. A slight increase in pointing error can produce a disproportionately large reduction in damage.

Laser beams deliver energy with a highly concentrated central hotspot. When the hotspot remains centred on the aim point, heating accumulates rapidly. As the spot moves away, the target receives less of the beam's peak intensity.
