## Introduction

One reason [THOR]({{ 'thor/' | relative_url }})’s kill mechanism is difficult to verify publicly is that there may be no single, universal drone response to high-power microwave exposure. Two aircraft flying side by side can experience the same microwave pulse yet suffer very different outcomes: one may crash immediately, another may reboot and continue flying, and a third may land safely and later return to service. Public demonstrations rarely disclose the exact drone models, electronics architecture, shielding measures, or hardening techniques involved, making it difficult for outside observers to determine whether a reported “defeat” reflects a broadly effective weapon or a vulnerability specific to a particular target.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://afresearchlab.com/counter-swarm-high-power-weapon/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: afresearchlab.com">[Air Force Research Laboratory]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">afresearchlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air Force Research LaboratoryTACTICAL HIGH POWER OPERATIONAL RESPONDER...September 16, 2019 — The system uses high power microwaves to c...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 16, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why One Drone May Fall and Another Recover

High-power microwave weapons do not strike all drones in the same way. Unlike a projectile that physically impacts a target, microwave effects depend on how electromagnetic energy enters and propagates through a particular electronic system.

Small variations can alter the outcome:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* Antenna placement and radio design.
* Internal wiring length and routing.
* Shielding around processors and power electronics.
* Circuit-board layout.
* Use of commercial versus military-grade components.
* Orientation of the aircraft relative to the incoming pulse.

</div>

Researchers studying microwave effects on unmanned aircraft have repeatedly found that electromagnetic coupling pathways strongly influence whether electronics experience a brief malfunction, a processor reset, or component damage. The same pulse can therefore produce different results across different drone designs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ITPS...51.1885M/abstract" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ui.adsabs.harvard.edu">[ADS Absorption+2MDPI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ui.adsabs.harvard.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ADS AbsorptionHigh-Power Microwave Pulse-Induced Failure on...by Q Mao · 2023 · Cited by 28 — In this article, we investigate the impact...</span></span></span>

This creates a verification problem. Public footage may show a drone falling from the sky after exposure, but without knowing the target’s internal design, it is difficult to determine whether most drones would react similarly or whether that particular aircraft happened to possess a highly vulnerable architecture.

## Consumer Drones Versus Hardened Systems

The largest survivability differences are likely to exist between inexpensive commercial drones and systems designed with electromagnetic resilience in mind.

Most consumer drones prioritise low weight, low cost, and compact electronics. They often contain densely packed commercial components connected by numerous conductive pathways that can unintentionally act as entry points for electromagnetic energy. Such systems are generally not designed around military electromagnetic survivability requirements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.mobilityengineeringtech.com/component/content/article/45802-afit-0006" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mobilityengineeringtech.com">[mobilityengineeringtech.com+2The Strategist]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mobilityengineeringtech.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hardening Unmanned Aerial Systems Against High-Power...May 1, 2022 — 14 May 2022 — High power microwave weapons use intentional electrom...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 1, 2022</span></span></span>

By contrast, military and specialised industrial systems may incorporate features intended to reduce vulnerability:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* Shielded electronic enclosures.
* Filtered power lines.
* Hardened processors.
* Improved grounding schemes.
* Protected communications systems.
* Redundant flight-control architectures.

</div>

The US Navy’s directed-energy research programme explicitly treats resilience against directed-energy threats as a technical challenge, illustrating that survivability can be engineered rather than assumed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves The ability to focus the radiated energy reliably and repeatedly</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesThe ability to focus the radiated energy reliably and repeatedly at...</span></span></span>

The implication for THOR demonstrations is straightforward: a successful engagement against lightly protected commercial drones does not automatically reveal how a hardened military unmanned system would perform under similar conditions.

## Temporary Upset Versus Permanent Damage

Another source of uncertainty is that microwave exposure does not always produce permanent destruction.

Government and defence literature commonly distinguishes between temporary degradation, mission defeat, and physical damage. High-power microwave effects can range from brief electronic upset to irreversible semiconductor failure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO+2Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — These weapons include high energy lasers and other high power electroma...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

A drone might experience:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* A processor reboot.
* Loss of communications.
* Navigation errors.
* Sensor malfunction.
* Flight-controller crashes.
* Permanent damage to electronic components.

</div>

From an observer’s perspective, several of these outcomes can appear identical. A drone that suddenly descends could be suffering catastrophic hardware failure, or it could simply have entered a programmed failsafe mode after a temporary disruption. Public demonstrations seldom provide post-flight forensic analysis showing which explanation applies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — These weapons include high energy lasers and other high power electroma...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because a temporary upset and permanent destruction imply very different operational consequences. A drone that can be restarted and flown again represents a fundamentally different level of effectiveness than one that requires replacement.

## Autonomy Changes the Outcome

The level of onboard autonomy can also affect survival.

Many commercial drones rely heavily on communications links, satellite navigation, and continuous interaction between sensors and flight-control software. If a microwave pulse disrupts one of these systems, the aircraft may become unstable or enter a safety mode.

More autonomous [platforms]({{ 'platforms/' | relative_url }}) may be able to tolerate certain disruptions because they can continue navigating or executing pre-programmed tasks even after losing some external inputs. Modern [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) discussions increasingly emphasise that autonomous systems may react differently to electromagnetic attack than remotely piloted aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08477" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

Consequently, observing that one drone crashes after exposure does not necessarily reveal how another platform with different software architecture would behave.

## Why Radio and Wiring Design Matter

Microwave weapons depend on delivering electromagnetic energy into vulnerable electronics. Antennas, cables, wiring harnesses, and conductive structures can all become pathways through which energy reaches sensitive components.

Studies of high-power microwave effects on unmanned aircraft consistently identify coupling through exposed wiring and electronic subsystems as a key factor in mission defeat. The amount of energy entering the aircraft depends on details that are rarely visible from the outside.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ITPS...51.1885M/abstract" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ui.adsabs.harvard.edu">[ADS Absorption+2arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ui.adsabs.harvard.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ADS AbsorptionHigh-Power Microwave Pulse-Induced Failure on...by Q Mao · 2023 · Cited by 28 — In this article, we investigate the impact...</span></span></span>

Two drones may have similar size and appearance while possessing radically different electromagnetic characteristics:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* One may have wiring that efficiently couples incoming energy.
* Another may have shorter cable runs and better shielding.
* One radio system may present a favourable entry path.
* Another may include filtering that attenuates the disturbance.

</div>

These hidden [engineering]({{ 'engineering/' | relative_url }}) differences can strongly influence survivability.

## Why Target Details Matter in Public Demonstrations

This variability explains why public counter-drone demonstrations often leave important questions unanswered.

When organisations announce that a high-power microwave system disabled a swarm, observers usually receive limited information about the targets. Critical details are often absent:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* Exact drone models.
* Internal electronics configuration.
* Hardening measures.
* Firmware versions.
* Autonomy levels.
* Shielding characteristics.
* Post-engagement forensic findings.

</div>

Without such information, it is difficult to determine whether the observed result reflects a broadly repeatable capability or a favourable match between the weapon and a particular class of drone.
