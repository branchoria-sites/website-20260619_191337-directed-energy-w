## Introduction

Leonidas became significantly more than a promising [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) demonstration when the U.S. Army incorporated it into the Indirect Fire Protection Capability–High Power Microwave (IFPC-HPM) programme. The key change was not technological alone; it was institutional. Through a sequence of prototype awards, deliveries, [soldier training]({{ 'soldier-training/' | relative_url }}) activities and follow-on contracts, the Army created a pathway that moved Leonidas from a test article into a funded capability with an operational user, developmental feedback loops and a roadmap for future upgrades. In the directed-energy weapons market, that transition is often the difference between an interesting prototype and a programme with long-term momentum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system-301727354.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prnewswire.com">[PR Newswire+2Breaking Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prnewswire.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PR Newswire U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...Epirus, a high-growth technology company, announced today a $66.1 million contract aw...</span></span></span>

## The 2023 IFPC-HPM Prototype Award

The decisive moment came in January 2023, when the Army's Rapid Capabilities and Critical Technologies Office (RCCTO) awarded Epirus a contract worth approximately $66.1 million to provide four high-power microwave prototypes for the IFPC-HPM effort. The programme was designed to explore a counter-swarm capability able to defend fixed and semi-fixed sites against groups of small unmanned aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system-301727354.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prnewswire.com">[PR Newswire+2Breaking Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prnewswire.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PR Newswire U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...Epirus, a high-growth technology company, announced today a $66.1 million contract aw...</span></span></span>

The award mattered for several reasons.

First, it represented a competitive government selection rather than a simple technology demonstration. Army reporting and industry accounts indicated that Leonidas emerged from a government evaluation process focused on counter-[drone swarm]({{ 'swarm-limits/' | relative_url }}) performance. The resulting contract gave the Army multiple systems to test rather than a single experimental unit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.potomacofficersclub.com/army-receives-epirus-leonidas-counter-uas-prototype/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: potomacofficersclub.com">[Potomac Officers Club]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">potomacofficersclub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">army receives epirus leonidas counter uas prototype</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Potomac Officers ClubUS Army Receives Epirus Leonidas Counter-UAS PrototypeNov 2, 2023 — Epirus built the Leonidas prototype under a $66...</span></span></span>

Second, the award placed Leonidas inside the Army's broader air-defence modernisation framework. IFPC is intended to fill gaps between traditional short-range air defence systems and larger missile-defence architectures. By attaching high-power microwave technology to IFPC, the Army effectively signalled that directed-energy approaches deserved serious examination as part of future layered defence networks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://taskandpurpose.com/news/army-microwave-weapon-drone-swarms-ifpc-hpm-contract/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: taskandpurpose.com">[Task &amp; Purpose]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">taskandpurpose.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">army microwave weapon drone swarms ifpc hpm contract</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Task &amp; PurposeArmy awards Epirus contract for counter-drone microwave...Jan 26, 2023 — The Army had requested $131.09 million for fiscal...</span></span></span>

Third, the contract created a funded production and testing cycle. Instead of asking whether Leonidas could work in principle, the Army began evaluating whether it could be manufactured, delivered, trained on, maintained and integrated into military operations. That shift is often where many defence technologies fail; in Leonidas' case, it became a source of momentum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system-301727354.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prnewswire.com">[PR Newswire+2Breaking Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prnewswire.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PR Newswire U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...Epirus, a high-growth technology company, announced today a $66.1 million contract aw...</span></span></span>

## Delivery, Training and Developmental Testing

Winning a prototype contract is only the first step. The more important test is whether a system survives the Army's implementation process.

In November 2023, Epirus delivered the first IFPC-HPM prototype derived from Leonidas. The delivery followed government acceptance testing and provided the Army with its first opportunity to begin handling the capability as military equipment rather than a contractor-owned demonstration asset.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://breakingdefense.com/2023/11/army-gets-its-hands-on-new-high-power-microwave-prototype-from-epirus/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: breakingdefense.com">[Breaking Defense+2Defense News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">breakingdefense.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">army gets its hands on new high power microwave prototype from epirus</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Breaking DefenseArmy gets its hands on new high-power microwave...1 Nov 2023 — The delivery marks the first of four prototypes derived f...</span></span></span>

By spring 2024, all four contracted systems had been delivered. Equally important, the programme completed New Equipment Training (NET) and Engineering Developmental Testing (EDT), milestones that are frequently overlooked outside defence circles but are critical to programme maturation. NET familiarises soldiers with operation and maintenance procedures, while EDT examines system performance, reliability and suitability under military testing conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-finalizes-delivery-of-four-ifpc-hpm-systems-to-u-s-army-completes-new-equipment-training-and-engineering-developmental-testing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Finalizes Delivery of Four IFPC-HPM Systems to...15 May 2024 — Epirus announced today that it has delivered the final two o...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

## Why Army Implementation Changed the Market View

For the anti-drone sector, the significance of the Army programme extended beyond the immediate contract value.

Many counter-UAS technologies can demonstrate effectiveness during controlled events. Far fewer obtain an operational sponsor willing to invest in logistics, training, testing infrastructure and future upgrades. The IFPC-HPM effort gave Leonidas exactly those advantages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system-301727354.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prnewswire.com">[PR Newswire]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prnewswire.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PR Newswire U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...Epirus, a high-growth technology company, announced today a $66.1 million contract aw...</span></span></span>

The clearest evidence that Leonidas had progressed beyond a prototype experiment arrived in 2025.

## What the Contracts Reveal About Leonidas' Trajectory

The Army's engagement with Leonidas shows how defence adoption often occurs through incremental implementation rather than sudden procurement decisions.

The 2023 prototype award established funding and formal evaluation. Deliveries in 2023 and 2024 created real equipment for Army testing and training. Developmental testing generated performance data and operational feedback. The 2025 Generation II award then converted those lessons into an upgraded capability with additional Army investment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-army-awards-epirus-66-1m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system-301727354.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prnewswire.com">[PR Newswire+2Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prnewswire.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PR Newswire U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army Awards Epirus $66.1M Contract for Leonidas™...Epirus, a high-growth technology company, announced today a $66.1 million contract aw...</span></span></span>
