## Introduction

A destroyer equipped with HELIOS does not simply gain a new weapon. It gains a new choice. Before an integrated laser entered the Aegis combat system, commanders facing a small drone or similar aerial threat often had to choose between electronic warfare, naval guns, close-in weapons, or expensive missile interceptors. HELIOS adds another option that can be selected through the same combat architecture that already manages sensors, tracks and weapons. The practical result is not that [lasers]({{ 'lasers/' | relative_url }}) replace missiles, but that Aegis can match threats with a broader range of responses, helping preserve scarce and costly interceptors for the targets that truly require them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/aegis-combat-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinAegis Combat SystemAEGIS Weapon System is a centralized, automated, command-and-control (C2) and weapons control system de...</span></span></span>

## The Response Options Before HELIOS

Modern destroyers already carry multiple defensive tools, each with advantages and drawbacks.

Against a suspicious aerial contact, commanders may first attempt electronic measures such as jamming or sensor disruption. If the threat continues, the ship can engage with guns, close-in weapon systems, or surface-to-air missiles. The problem is that these weapons differ dramatically in cost, range, magazine depth and suitability. A missile may offer the highest probability of kill against a dangerous target, but it is also a finite and expensive resource. Guns are cheaper but have shorter effective engagement windows and depend on ammunition carried aboard.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2024-07-24_R44175_bd7aad12b8fb1d1589b9506a55c9ad6922b142a3.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: everycrsreport.com">[Every CRS Report+2Business Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">everycrsreport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Every CRS ReportNavy Shipboard Lasers: Background and Issues for...24 Jul 2024 — High-energy SSLs on Navy ships would generally be short...</span></span></span>

The rise of inexpensive drones and unmanned systems has intensified this problem. An attacker can launch large numbers of relatively cheap aerial threats, forcing defenders to decide whether each target justifies the expenditure of a missile interceptor. Analysts and naval officials have repeatedly highlighted the danger of allowing low-cost threats to consume high-value defensive munitions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2022/10/19/navy-destroyer-adds-helios-laser-to-arsenal" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationaldefensemagazine.org">[National Defense Magazine+2defencesciencereview.com.pl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationaldefensemagazine.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">navy destroyer adds helios laser to arsenal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Defense MagazineNavy Destroyer Adds HELIOS Laser to Arsenal (UPDATED)19 Oct 2022 — Now, after decades of experimentation, the Na...</span></span></span>

Before HELIOS, there was a larger gap between non-kinetic measures and kinetic interceptors. A commander often had to move more quickly from observation and jamming to guns or missiles once a threat was judged dangerous enough to engage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2024-07-24_R44175_bd7aad12b8fb1d1589b9506a55c9ad6922b142a3.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: everycrsreport.com">[Every CRS Report]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">everycrsreport.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Every CRS ReportNavy Shipboard Lasers: Background and Issues for...24 Jul 2024 — High-energy SSLs on Navy ships would generally be short...</span></span></span>

## How Aegis Can Assign the Least Costly Useful Weapon

The Aegis Combat System was designed around threat evaluation and weapon assignment. It combines sensor information, assesses danger levels and helps determine which weapon should engage which target. HELIOS matters because it joins this existing decision framework rather than operating as an isolated experiment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/aegis-combat-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2NAVSEA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinAegis Combat SystemAEGIS Weapon System is a centralized, automated, command-and-control (C2) and weapons control system de...</span></span></span>

In practical terms, Aegis can now consider an additional response option when confronting a tracked target:

* Observe and classify the contact.
* Attempt non-destructive measures if appropriate.
* Use the laser's optical dazzling capability against sensors when that effect is sufficient.
* Employ the high-energy laser against suitable drones or similar threats.
* Reserve missiles and other high-value interceptors for more demanding targets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2021/2/15/navy-to-fully-integrate-laser-into-aegis-combat-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationaldefensemagazine.org">[National Defense Magazine+2Every CRS Report]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationaldefensemagazine.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">navy to fully integrate laser into aegis combat system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Defense MagazineNavy to Fully Integrate Laser into Aegis Combat System15 Feb 2021 — HELIOS is a 60-kilowatt solid-state laser ca...</span></span></span>

This matters because HELIOS offers what Lockheed Martin describes as a "precision response" with a [deep magazine]({{ 'deep-magazine/' | relative_url }}) and low cost per engagement. The ship does not need to launch a missile every time it confronts a lower-end aerial threat. Instead, commanders gain an intermediate option that may achieve the required effect while preserving missile inventory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2National Defense Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon System9 Jan 2021 — The HELIOS system&#x27;s deep magazine, low cost per kill...</span></span></span>

The key operational change is therefore economic as much as technical. A destroyer's missile cells are limited. Electrical power, while not unlimited, can support many more laser engagements than the number of interceptors physically carried aboard the ship. The laser changes the calculation from "Can we afford to spend a missile on this target?" to "What is the least costly weapon that can reliably achieve the desired effect?"<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2defencesciencereview.com.pl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon System9 Jan 2021 — The HELIOS system&#x27;s deep magazine, low cost per kill...</span></span></span>

## When Should a Destroyer Use a Laser?

The answer depends on the nature of the threat.

HELIOS is most attractive when the target is dangerous enough to require engagement but not so demanding that it immediately justifies a missile. Small drones are the clearest example. Such targets can threaten surveillance, targeting or ship safety, yet they may not warrant the expenditure of a sophisticated interceptor. HELIOS was specifically designed with [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) and counter-unmanned-aircraft missions in mind.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2021/2/15/navy-to-fully-integrate-laser-into-aegis-combat-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationaldefensemagazine.org">[National Defense Magazine+2Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationaldefensemagazine.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">navy to fully integrate laser into aegis combat system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Defense MagazineNavy to Fully Integrate Laser into Aegis Combat System15 Feb 2021 — HELIOS is a 60-kilowatt solid-state laser ca...</span></span></span>

Recent demonstrations illustrate this intended role. ## Why Lasers Fit Layered Naval Defence

Naval defence has long relied on layers. A ship seeks to defeat threats as far away as possible with long-range systems, while retaining progressively shorter-range options if earlier layers fail. HELIOS fits naturally into this structure because it adds another layer rather than replacing an existing one.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/aegis-combat-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinAegis Combat SystemAEGIS Weapon System is a centralized, automated, command-and-control (C2) and weapons control system de...</span></span></span>

A layered engagement sequence might involve:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Long-range detection and tracking through Aegis sensors.
* Electronic warfare and sensor disruption.
* Laser dazzling or laser engagement against suitable targets.
* Gun systems and close-in weapons.
* Missile interceptors where required.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2021/2/15/navy-to-fully-integrate-laser-into-aegis-combat-system" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationaldefensemagazine.org">[National Defense Magazine+2Every CRS Report]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationaldefensemagazine.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">navy to fully integrate laser into aegis combat system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Defense MagazineNavy to Fully Integrate Laser into Aegis Combat System15 Feb 2021 — HELIOS is a 60-kilowatt solid-state laser ca...</span></span></span>

The value of HELIOS becomes most apparent during [saturation]({{ 'saturation/' | relative_url }}) scenarios involving numerous inexpensive threats. In such situations, every missile saved can remain available for a more capable aircraft, cruise missile or future attack wave. Lasers therefore contribute not only by destroying targets but by helping the ship manage its limited defensive resources across an entire engagement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2022/10/19/navy-destroyer-adds-helios-laser-to-arsenal" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationaldefensemagazine.org">[National Defense Magazine+2defencesciencereview.com.pl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationaldefensemagazine.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">navy destroyer adds helios laser to arsenal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Defense MagazineNavy Destroyer Adds HELIOS Laser to Arsenal (UPDATED)19 Oct 2022 — Now, after decades of experimentation, the Na...</span></span></span>

That is why HELIOS changes weapon choice on a destroyer. The laser is not primarily important because it fires a beam of light. It is important because, once integrated into Aegis, it gives commanders a new middle option between doing nothing and firing an expensive interceptor. In an era of proliferating drones and other low-cost aerial threats, that additional choice can be as significant as the weapon itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2National Defense Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon System9 Jan 2021 — The HELIOS system&#x27;s deep magazine, low cost per kill...</span></span></span>
