## Introduction

The 49-drone Leonidas test at Camp Atterbury was one of the most eye-catching directed-energy demonstrations yet conducted in the [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) sector. In the event, Epirus’s Leonidas high-power microwave system disabled a swarm of 49 drones simultaneously, part of a broader live-fire demonstration in which the company reported neutralising all 61 drones flown across several scenarios. The result mattered because it provided visible evidence that a microwave-based system could engage many drones at once rather than defeating them one by one. What it did not prove, however, was that such performance will automatically translate to every [battlefield]({{ 'battle-limits/' | relative_url }}) environment, drone type, weather condition or operational scenario. The real significance of the test lies in understanding exactly what evidence it generated—and where the remaining uncertainties begin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-...10 Sept 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49-drone...</span></span></span>

## What Epirus Claimed From the Live-Fire Event

The demonstration took place at Camp [Atterbury]({{ 'open-questions-f1b729/' | relative_url }}) in Indiana before representatives from the US military, government agencies and allied nations. According to Epirus, Leonidas successfully defeated all 61 drones flown during five different engagement scenarios, culminating in the destruction of a 49-[drone swarm]({{ 'swarm-limits/' | relative_url }}) with a single pulse of electromagnetic interference. The company characterised the event as proof of a “one-to-many” counter-swarm capability rather than a conventional one-target-at-a-time air-defence engagement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-...10 Sept 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49-drone...</span></span></span>

Several aspects of the demonstration were noteworthy:

* A large swarm was engaged simultaneously rather than sequentially.
* Multiple drone scenarios were reportedly tested, including swarm engagements, selective targeting and controlled intercepts.
* The event demonstrated the operational concept behind high-power microwave weapons: disrupting electronics rather than physically striking targets.

## What the Demonstration Actually Proved

The strongest conclusion supported by the available evidence is that Leonidas demonstrated a credible counter-swarm effect under controlled live-fire conditions.

That may sound modest, but it is significant. Counter-swarm defence is one of the hardest challenges in modern military technology because the problem is not simply detecting drones; it is defeating many of them quickly enough to matter. A system that can affect dozens of targets with a single engagement has a fundamentally different operating model from guns, missiles or many electronic-warfare systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

The test therefore provided evidence for three propositions:

**First, Leonidas can generate operationally relevant microwave effects against airborne drones.** The drones were not theoretical targets or laboratory electronics; they were flying aircraft in a live demonstration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-...10 Sept 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49-drone...</span></span></span>

**Second, the system appears capable of affecting multiple drones simultaneously.** The 49-drone event is important precisely because it moves beyond demonstrations against single aircraft. A swarm defeat is the capability the system is being sold to provide.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-...10 Sept 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49-drone...</span></span></span>

**Third, the engagement appears repeatable across more than one scenario.** Epirus did not showcase only a single headline event; it reported several different engagement types, including selective targeting and multi-directional swarm engagements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=na0pGfopahM" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: youtube.com">[YouTube]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">youtube.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Neutralizes 61-of-61...The Leonidas high-power microwave technology platform is changing th...</span></span></span>

They can produce measurable effects against real drones in realistic test environments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus&#x27; Leonidas High-Power Microwave Defeats 49-...10 Sept 2025 — Leonidas neutralized 61-of-61 drones, culminating in a 49-drone...</span></span></span>

## Why Controlled Demonstrations Are Not Battlefield Proof

The strongest criticism of the 49-drone test is not that it failed. Rather, it is that successful demonstrations answer only some of the questions military users care about.

A live-fire event is designed to reveal capability, but it usually does not replicate the full complexity of combat. Public reports provide limited detail regarding drone hardening, electronic protection measures, environmental conditions, terrain effects, electromagnetic clutter, operator stress, [maintenance]({{ 'maintenance/' | relative_url }}) demands or long-term reliability. Without those details, outside observers cannot determine how broadly the results can be generalised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.axios.com/2025/08/28/epirus-indiana-drone-swarm-demo-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: axios.com">[Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">axios.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The event highlighted a significant advancement in counter-drone technology amid growing global security concerns over drone swarms, whic...</span></span></span>

Several unanswered questions remain:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Would the same effect occur against drones built with different electronics and shielding?
* How does performance change in rain, dust, urban environments or mountainous terrain?
* Can the system sustain repeated engagements over extended operations?
* How vulnerable is it to adversary adaptation once its characteristics become widely understood?
* What engagement ranges were achieved during the demonstration, and under what conditions?<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.axios.com/2025/08/28/epirus-indiana-drone-swarm-demo-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: axios.com">[Axios]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">axios.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The event highlighted a significant advancement in counter-drone technology amid growing global security concerns over drone swarms, whic...</span></span></span>

</div>

This distinction matters because military history is full of systems that performed impressively during demonstrations but encountered unexpected challenges once exposed to operational realities. The Camp Atterbury event demonstrated capability; it did not eliminate uncertainty.

## The Most Important Result Was Economic, Not Just Technical

The test also carried an economic message.

Modern conflicts have repeatedly shown how inexpensive drones can force defenders to expend much more expensive interceptors. A successful microwave engagement against dozens of drones suggests a different cost equation. If one pulse can disable many targets, the defender may avoid firing dozens of separate interceptors. That prospect is one reason the US Army and other defence organisations have shown sustained interest in high-power microwave programmes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>

## What Evidence Would Strengthen the Case Next

The next stage of evidence is more important than additional headline drone counts.

Military buyers are likely to place greater weight on demonstrations that show consistent performance under increasingly demanding conditions. Useful evidence would include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* Independent government test results rather than company-reported outcomes alone.
* Operational deployments with military units.
* Performance against diverse drone designs and electronics architectures.
* Data from challenging weather and terrain conditions.
* Evidence of repeated engagements over sustained operational periods.
* Results against coordinated attacks employing multiple drone types simultaneously.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_Leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epirus Leonidas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epirus Leonidas</span></span></span>
