## Introduction

High-power microwave (HPM) weapons occupy a distinctive niche within directed-energy air defence. Unlike [lasers]({{ 'lasers/' | relative_url }}), which concentrate energy on a specific point, microwave systems are designed to disrupt or damage electronics across a wider volume of space. That characteristic makes them especially attractive against drone [swarms]({{ 'swarms/' | relative_url }}), where dozens of aircraft may approach simultaneously and where engaging each target individually can be too slow or too expensive. At the same time, the very feature that makes HPM systems useful against groups of drones creates a governance challenge: electronics belonging to friendly forces, civilian infrastructure, or nearby systems may also be exposed if controls are inadequate. The result is a technology whose military value depends as much on rules, deconfliction and electromagnetic discipline as on raw power.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

## Why Microwave Systems Suit Groups of Drones

Drone swarms create a fundamentally different defensive problem from a single aircraft. Traditional air-defence systems often engage one target at a time and consume finite ammunition or missiles. A swarm seeks to overwhelm that logic through numbers. High-power microwave weapons attempt to reverse the cost equation by affecting many electronic targets within a beam footprint at once.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.droneshield.com/blog/a-counter-to-drone-swarms-high-power-microwave-weapons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: droneshield.com">[DroneShield]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">droneshield.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Counter to Drone Swarms: High-Power Microwave...Military forces must prioritise a counter to drone swarming tactics with wh...</span></span></span>

The mechanism is straightforward in concept. An HPM system emits intense electromagnetic energy intended to couple into electronic components, wiring or circuits. The objective is not necessarily to burn through a structure but to disrupt, degrade or permanently damage the electronics that allow a drone to navigate and function. The US Office of Naval Research describes HPM weapons as systems that interact directly with electronics, causing disruption or damage sufficient to prevent mission completion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onr.navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onr.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

For swarm defence, several advantages stand out:

* **One-to-many engagement:** A sufficiently broad microwave footprint can affect multiple drones during a single engagement cycle rather than requiring separate aim points.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.droneshield.com/blog/a-counter-to-drone-swarms-high-power-microwave-weapons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: droneshield.com">[DroneShield]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">droneshield.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Counter to Drone Swarms: High-Power Microwave...Military forces must prioritise a counter to drone swarming tactics with wh...</span></span></span>
* **Rapid response:** Electronic effects occur at electromagnetic speeds, potentially allowing defenders to engage dense formations before they disperse.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onr.navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onr.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>
* **Magazine depth:** The limiting factor is typically power generation and thermal management rather than stocks of interceptors.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defencefinancemonitor.com/p/non-kinetic-counter-uas-with-high" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defencefinancemonitor.com">[Defence Finance Monitor]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defencefinancemonitor.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">non kinetic counter uas with high</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defence Finance MonitorNon-Kinetic Counter-UAS with High Power Microwave8 Dec 2025 — This report examines the operational, technical and...</span></span></span>
* **Economics:** Defenders can avoid spending costly missiles against inexpensive mass-produced drones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.droneshield.com/blog/a-counter-to-drone-swarms-high-power-microwave-weapons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: droneshield.com">[DroneShield]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">droneshield.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Counter to Drone Swarms: High-Power Microwave...Military forces must prioritise a counter to drone swarming tactics with wh...</span></span></span>

In 2025, Epirus reported that its Leonidas HPM system neutralised dozens of drones during live-fire events, including a large swarm engagement in which a single microwave pulse reportedly affected 49 drones. ## How Area Effects Create Friendly-Risk Problems

The same area effects that make microwave weapons effective against swarms also create their most important limitation. Electronics do not inherently distinguish between friendly and hostile ownership.

The US Government Accountability Office has highlighted this issue directly, noting that wider-beam directed-energy systems such as high-power microwave weapons can affect all assets within an area, whether friend or foe. In practical terms, this means a commander cannot assume that only the targeted drones will experience disruption.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

Several categories of risk arise:

**Friendly military systems.** Modern forces rely heavily on digital communications, sensors, radios, networking equipment, navigation aids and autonomous systems. If these assets lie within the microwave engagement footprint, they may experience unintended effects unless appropriately hardened or excluded from the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.electronicsforu.com/market-verticals/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: electronicsforu.com">[Electronics For You]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">electronicsforu.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Electronics For YouDirected Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves4 Oct 2016 — Proper planning before an HPM attack could prevent unintend...</span></span></span>

**Civil infrastructure.** Military operations increasingly occur near ports, airfields, energy facilities and communications networks. The broader the microwave effect, the greater the need to understand nearby electronic dependencies before employment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qinetiq.com/-/media/0b9c065bcb994265975c27b3b6356cbb.ashx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qinetiq.com">[QinetiQ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qinetiq.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The challenges of Directed Energy30 Mar 2021 — Understanding collateral effects and interoperability with infrastructure is key. –...</span></span></span>

**Uncertain damage assessment.** Microwave effects are often less visually obvious than kinetic interception. A drone may lose function and fall, but evaluating whether nearby systems were also affected can be more difficult. Analysts have long noted that post-engagement assessment for HPM attacks is inherently challenging because physical destruction may not be visible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.electronicsforu.com/market-verticals/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: electronicsforu.com">[Electronics For You]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">electronicsforu.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Electronics For YouDirected Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves4 Oct 2016 — Proper planning before an HPM attack could prevent unintend...</span></span></span>

**Mixed airspace complications.** Swarms may operate near friendly drones, civilian aircraft support systems or allied [platforms]({{ 'platforms/' | relative_url }}). ## The Governance Challenge Is Not Secondary

For microwave defence systems, governance is not simply a legal afterthought. It is part of the weapon's operational effectiveness.

A laser operator generally attempts to place energy on a defined point. An HPM operator must instead think about an electromagnetic volume. The question is not merely whether the target lies within the beam but also what else lies within or near the affected area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

This changes how military organisations approach deployment. Before fielding microwave systems, planners typically need to address:

* The location of friendly communications and sensor networks.
* Electromagnetic compatibility with existing military equipment.
* Safety distances and exclusion zones.
* Procedures for operations near civilian infrastructure.
* Criteria for switching between microwave, electronic warfare and kinetic options.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qinetiq.com/-/media/0b9c065bcb994265975c27b3b6356cbb.ashx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qinetiq.com">[QinetiQ+2arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qinetiq.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The challenges of Directed Energy30 Mar 2021 — Understanding collateral effects and interoperability with infrastructure is key. –...</span></span></span>

## Rules and Deconfliction Before Field Use

Because of these risks, microwave weapons are most credible when integrated into a larger defensive architecture rather than treated as stand-alone solutions.

Effective deconfliction generally begins before an engagement occurs. Defenders must know which friendly systems are operating nearby, which areas are electronically sensitive, and whether alternative defensive tools are available. In many scenarios, commanders may choose a missile, gun, jammer or laser instead of a microwave system if collateral electronic exposure appears unacceptable.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>
