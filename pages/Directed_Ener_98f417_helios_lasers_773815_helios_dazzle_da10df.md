## Introduction

HELIOS is often described as a naval laser that can shoot down drones, but one of its most important functions operates below the threshold of physical destruction. The system’s name itself—High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzler and Surveillance—highlights that it is designed not only to burn targets but also to interfere with hostile optical sensors. In many situations, preventing an enemy drone, camera, seeker or reconnaissance system from seeing effectively can be enough to neutralise the threat without destroying the platform carrying it. This “dazzler” role gives commanders a reversible, scalable response option that sits between observation and lethal engagement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon System9 Jan 2021 — The High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzler...</span></span></span>

## When Blinding Sensors Beats Burning Drones

### What optical dazzling is meant to do

Optical dazzling is a directed-energy technique that aims to overwhelm or disrupt a sensor rather than physically destroy the entire platform carrying it. Instead of concentrating energy long enough to burn through structures, the laser directs controlled light into optical systems such as cameras, infrared imagers or optical seekers. The result can range from temporary glare and image washout to sensor [saturation]({{ 'saturation/' | relative_url }}) or, in some circumstances, permanent damage to sensitive detector components.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/us-navys-answer-sea-launched-drones-lasers-199479" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalinterest.org">[The National Interest+2The War Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalinterest.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Interest The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy&#x27;s Answer To Sea-launched Drones: LasersJanuary 19, 2022 — “The laser is meant to zap electro-optical sensors, like digital video cam...</span></span></span>

The key distinction is that the target of the attack is information rather than the vehicle itself. A drone whose camera can no longer provide useful imagery may become ineffective even if its airframe remains intact. Likewise, a surveillance system that cannot maintain a clear picture of a naval vessel loses much of its military value. HELIOS incorporates this concept directly into its design through its integrated optical dazzler capability.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon System9 Jan 2021 — The High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzler...</span></span></span>

This approach aligns with a broader trend in directed-energy development. Military planners increasingly view sensors as critical vulnerabilities because modern warfare relies heavily on cameras, infrared systems and electro-optical targeting equipment. Disrupting those systems can produce operational effects disproportionate to the amount of energy used.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalacademies.org/projects/DEPS-DEPS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalacademies.org">[National Academies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalacademies.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National AcademiesReview of Advancements in Active Electro-Optical Systems...Evaluate the fundamental, physical limits to active electro...</span></span></span>

## Why Drones and Cameras Are Attractive Targets

Modern unmanned aerial systems are particularly vulnerable to dazzling because many depend on optical payloads for navigation, reconnaissance and targeting. Operators often require live video feeds to identify objects, maintain situational awareness or guide missions. If the sensor feed becomes unusable, the aircraft may lose much of its tactical utility even though it remains airborne.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/us-navys-answer-sea-launched-drones-lasers-199479" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalinterest.org">[The National Interest]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalinterest.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Interest The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy&#x27;s Answer To Sea-launched Drones: LasersJanuary 19, 2022 — “The laser is meant to zap electro-optical sensors, like digital video cam...</span></span></span>

Several factors make sensors appealing targets:

* **Sensors are fragile compared with airframes.** Cameras, infrared detectors and optical seekers contain sensitive components that can be disrupted by relatively modest laser effects.
* **A single sensor may be mission-critical.** Many small drones rely on only a few cameras or imaging systems.
* **Reconnaissance [platforms]({{ 'platforms/' | relative_url }}) depend on observation.** If a surveillance drone cannot collect imagery, its intelligence value collapses.
* **Optical seekers can be degraded.** Weapons or platforms that rely on electro-optical guidance may become less effective if their sensors are blinded or confused.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/us-navys-answer-sea-launched-drones-lasers-199479" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalinterest.org">[The National Interest+2The War Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalinterest.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Interest The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy&#x27;s Answer To Sea-launched Drones: LasersJanuary 19, 2022 — “The laser is meant to zap electro-optical sensors, like digital video cam...</span></span></span>

The US Navy and defence analysts have repeatedly highlighted this sensor-focused use case. Descriptions of HELIOS and related naval dazzler systems emphasise disruption of electro-optical and infrared sensors on hostile platforms rather than immediate destruction of the platforms themselves.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2022/10/19/navy-destroyer-adds-helios-laser-to-arsenal" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationaldefensemagazine.org">[National Defense Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationaldefensemagazine.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">navy destroyer adds helios laser to arsenal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Defense MagazineNavy Destroyer Adds HELIOS Laser to Arsenal (UPDATED)19 Oct 2022 — The Optical Dazzling Interdictor Navy, or ODI...</span></span></span>

## How HELIOS Fits Into Naval Escalation Control

A warship does not always want its next action to be a missile launch or the destruction of another vessel’s equipment. Maritime operations often occur in crowded environments involving ambiguous contacts, intelligence-gathering platforms and unmanned systems operating in legally or politically sensitive circumstances.

The dazzler function gives commanders an intermediate response. Instead of escalating directly to kinetic force, a ship can interfere with hostile surveillance or targeting activities. This creates a spectrum of options:

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

1. Detect and track the contact.
2. Issue warnings or manoeuvre.
3. Disrupt sensors through dazzling.
4. Escalate to destructive laser effects if necessary.
5. Employ guns or missiles against higher-end threats.

</div>

This layered response structure is one reason naval analysts describe dazzling as useful for “grey-zone” competition and other situations where commanders seek to counter surveillance without immediately destroying a target.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pressbooks.pub/maritimesecurity11/chapter/naval-advanced-weapons-systems-puntoriero/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pressbooks.pub">[Pressbooks]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pressbooks.pub</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval Advanced Weapons Systems (Puntoriero</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval Advanced Weapons Systems (Puntoriero)September 18, 2025 — by J Beckman · 2025 — By adding a non-kinetic dazzler effect, H...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 18, 2025</span></span></span>

The capability is particularly valuable because reconnaissance often precedes attack. A drone gathering targeting information or maintaining visual contact may be degraded before it can contribute effectively to a strike chain. In that sense, dazzling attacks the threat’s ability to observe rather than waiting to defeat the threat after it has already acted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/us-navys-answer-sea-launched-drones-lasers-199479" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalinterest.org">[The National Interest]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalinterest.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Interest The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy&#x27;s Answer To Sea-launched Drones: LasersJanuary 19, 2022 — “The laser is meant to zap electro-optical sensors, like digital video cam...</span></span></span>

## Why Integration Matters More Than a Stand-Alone Dazzler

The Navy has fielded optical dazzling capabilities before, notably through the Optical Dazzling Interdictor Navy (ODIN) programme. ## The Practical Limits of Sensor Dazzling

Although dazzling can be highly effective, it is not a universal solution. Modern military systems may employ multiple sensors, protective filters, alternative navigation methods or autonomous behaviours that reduce dependence on a single camera. Environmental conditions also matter. Atmospheric effects, range, target movement and optical design all influence how effectively laser energy reaches a sensor.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalacademies.org/projects/DEPS-DEPS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalacademies.org">[National Academies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalacademies.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National AcademiesReview of Advancements in Active Electro-Optical Systems...Evaluate the fundamental, physical limits to active electro...</span></span></span>

For that reason, the dazzler role should be understood as one tool within a layered naval defence architecture. Its strength lies in creating operational effects at lower levels of force, especially against surveillance and reconnaissance systems that depend on seeing clearly.

## Why the Dazzler Role Matters

The most revealing aspect of HELIOS may not be its ability to burn through targets but its recognition that information is often the real objective. Modern drones, reconnaissance systems and sensor-equipped platforms derive their value from what they can observe and transmit. If a warship can deny that vision, it may achieve its defensive goal without destroying the platform itself.

That makes the optical dazzler function more than a secondary feature. It reflects a broader shift in directed-energy warfare: defeating an enemy’s sensors can sometimes be faster, cheaper and more politically flexible than defeating the entire target. HELIOS embodies that idea by giving naval commanders a means to attack the eyes of a threat before they need to attack the threat itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pressbooks.pub/maritimesecurity11/chapter/naval-advanced-weapons-systems-puntoriero/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pressbooks.pub">[Pressbooks+2National Defense Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pressbooks.pub</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval Advanced Weapons Systems (Puntoriero</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval Advanced Weapons Systems (Puntoriero)September 18, 2025 — by J Beckman · 2025 — By adding a non-kinetic dazzler effect, H...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 18, 2025</span></span></span>
