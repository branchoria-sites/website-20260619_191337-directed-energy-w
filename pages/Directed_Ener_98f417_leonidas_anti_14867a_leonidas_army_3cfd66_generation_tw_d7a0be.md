## Introduction

The strongest signal yet that Leonidas may be moving beyond the usual cycle of directed-energy demonstrations is not a test video or a technical claim. It is the U.S. Army’s decision to fund a second-generation version after completing delivery, training and developmental testing of the first four systems. In July 2025, the Army’s Rapid Capabilities and Critical Technologies Office (RCCTO) awarded Epirus a $43.5 million contract for two IFPC-HPM Generation II systems based on the Leonidas high-power microwave platform. Rather than restarting from scratch, the Army chose to invest in an upgraded follow-on design informed by operational feedback from the first generation. That decision suggests growing institutional confidence, but it does not yet guarantee large-scale procurement or a formal acquisition programme.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-receives-43-million-contract-from-u-s-army-for-ifpc-hpm-generation-ii-systems" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army for...17 Jul 2025 — Epirus Receives $43.5 Million Contract from U.S. Army for IFPC-HPM Generation II Systems... ## Did Leonidas Escape Prototype Limbo?

Many directed-energy projects reach a successful demonstration and then stall. They may perform well in trials yet fail to attract sustained funding, operational sponsorship or a pathway toward procurement. The Generation II contract matters because it represents a follow-on investment after the Army had already received, trained on and evaluated the first systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.jedonline.com/2024/11/25/epirus-secures-ifpc-hpm-upgrade-contract-from-us-army/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jedonline.com">[Journal of Electromagnetic Dominance]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jedonline.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Journal of Electromagnetic DominanceEpirus Secures IFPC-HPM Upgrade Contract from US ArmyNovember 25, 2024 — 25 Nov 2024 — The RCCTO in J...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 25, 2024</span></span></span>

The Army's original 2023 contract funded four IFPC-HPM prototypes. By 2024, those systems had been delivered and subjected to [engineering]({{ 'engineering/' | relative_url }}) developmental testing, live-fire activities and operational assessments. The Generation II award explicitly states that the upgraded systems will incorporate soldier feedback and lessons from those events. That is a different stage of maturity from a technology demonstration; it indicates a feedback loop between users and developers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-receives-43-million-contract-from-u-s-army-for-ifpc-hpm-generation-ii-systems" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army for...17 Jul 2025 — Epirus Receives $43.5 Million Contract from U.S. Army for IFPC-HPM Generation II Systems... In acquisition terms, this is evidence of momentum rather than proof of success. The Army did not simply renew support for the same design. It funded a next increment intended to solve shortcomings identified during real testing. ## What Changed Between the First and Second Contracts?

The most revealing aspect of the Generation II contract is not its dollar value but its emphasis on performance improvements.

The first-generation effort focused on proving that a high-power microwave system could contribute to [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) defence within the Indirect Fire Protection Capability framework. The second-generation effort is focused on increasing effectiveness and military utility. According to Epirus and Army-related reporting, the new systems are expected to:

* More than double the effective range of Generation I.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://defence-industry.eu/epirus-awarded-43-5-million-u-s-army-contract-for-leonidas-generation-ii-high-power-microwave-system/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defence-industry.eu">[defence-industry.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defence-industry.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Defence Industry Epirus awarded $43.5 million U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army contract for...20 Jul 2025 — The upgraded GEN II systems are expected to more than double the effective range of their predecessors...</span></span></span>

* Incorporate higher-density battery systems.
* Reduce dependence on external power sources.
* Improve multi-target engagement performance.

These goals reveal what the Army apparently learned from earlier testing. The question was no longer whether Leonidas could disrupt drones. The question became whether it could do so from greater distances, for longer periods and with less logistical burden. That shift is characteristic of programmes that are beginning to focus on operational usefulness rather than laboratory validation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-receives-43-million-contract-from-u-s-army-for-ifpc-hpm-generation-ii-systems" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army for...17 Jul 2025 — Epirus Receives $43.5 Million Contract from U.S. Army for IFPC-HPM Generation II Systems... ## Why Range, Power and Usability Became Upgrade Goals

The Generation II requirements expose some of the practical challenges facing directed-energy weapons.

### Range Matters More Than Demonstrations

A counter-drone system that works only at relatively short distances may struggle to fit into layered air-defence architectures. The Army's stated interest in more than doubling effective range suggests that existing performance, while promising, left room for improvement when confronting realistic operational scenarios.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-receives-43-million-contract-from-u-s-army-for-ifpc-hpm-generation-ii-systems" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Army for...17 Jul 2025 — Epirus Receives $43.5 Million Contract from U.S. Army for IFPC-HPM Generation II Systems... Longer range increases engagement opportunities, expands defended areas and potentially allows a smaller number of systems to protect larger sites. It also helps address a recurring criticism of directed-energy technologies: impressive demonstrations do not always translate into tactically useful standoff distances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.tectonicdefense.com/epirus-secures-43-5m-from-army-rccto/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tectonicdefense.com">[Tectonic Defense]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tectonicdefense.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">epirus secures 43 5m from army rccto</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tectonic DefenseEpirus Secures $43.5M from Army RCCTO17 Jul 2025 — Epirus won a $43.5M Army RCCTO contract to deliver GEN II Leonidas cou...</span></span></span>

### Power and Lethality Are Linked

The planned increase in power is tied to a broader goal of improving effectiveness against more threat types. Counter-drone environments are evolving rapidly, with larger unmanned aircraft, more sophisticated electronics and increasingly complex swarm behaviours. ## Why the Army Is Still Being Careful

The Generation II contract is encouraging for Leonidas, but its language also reveals caution.
