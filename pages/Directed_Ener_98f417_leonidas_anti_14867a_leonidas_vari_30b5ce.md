## Introduction

Leonidas has become more than a single [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) system. One of the most important developments in the directed-energy market is the way Epirus has adapted the same high-power microwave architecture across multiple platforms and missions. Instead of treating counter-drone defence as a fixed installation or a single military programme, the company has steadily reshaped Leonidas for armoured vehicles, [expeditionary]({{ 'marine-variant/' | relative_url }}) forces, maritime security and even autonomous operations. The result is a family of systems that demonstrates how a microwave effect originally associated with drone-swarm defence can be packaged for different services and operational environments. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

For the anti-drone market, this matters because platform flexibility often determines commercial success. Leonidas’ variants therefore reveal as much about defence acquisition trends as they do about microwave technology itself. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/electronic-warfare" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on epirusinc.com.</span></span></span>

## Vehicle-Mounted Stryker Integration

The first major expansion beyond the base Leonidas concept was its integration with the [Stryker]({{ 'stryker/' | relative_url }}) armoured vehicle. In 2022, Epirus and General Dynamics Land Systems publicly unveiled a working prototype that mounted the Leonidas high-power microwave array on a Stryker platform. The integration followed a strategic partnership intended to combine microwave counter-electronics effects with a combat vehicle already familiar to US Army operators. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

The significance of Stryker Leonidas was not simply mobility. Earlier counter-drone systems often required separate trailers, fixed sites or specialised support equipment. Mounting Leonidas on a combat vehicle placed the microwave weapon inside a manoeuvre-force context, allowing protection to move with troops rather than remaining tied to a defended location. This reflected growing military concern that drone threats would accompany forces throughout a [battlefield]({{ 'battle-limits/' | relative_url }}) rather than appear only around static bases. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

Historically, Stryker Leonidas also helped demonstrate the scalability of Epirus’ architecture. Rather than redesigning the underlying microwave effect, the company adapted it to an existing military vehicle ecosystem. That approach reduced integration risk and provided a visible example of how high-power microwave systems might fit into future short-range air-defence formations. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-general-dynamics-land-systems-unveil-integrated-counter-electronics-system-stryker-leonidas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus, General Dynamics Land Systems Unveil Integrated...5 Oct 2022 — The companies have successfully integrated Epirus&#x27; high-pow...</span></span></span>

## Why the Marine Corps Wanted a Smaller Leonidas

The next major step was Leonidas Expeditionary, developed through the Expeditionary Directed Energy Counter-Swarm (ExDECS) programme. While Stryker Leonidas emphasised vehicle integration for manoeuvre forces, the Marine Corps sought something lighter, easier to deploy and better suited to expeditionary operations. The resulting system retained the core microwave technology but was packaged for use with light tactical vehicles, trailers and other mobile expeditionary [platforms]({{ 'platforms/' | relative_url }}). <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://defensescoop.com/2024/09/23/marines-microwave-weapon-hpm-epirus-leonidas-expeditionary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defensescoop.com">[DefenseScoop+2Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defensescoop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">marines microwave weapon hpm epirus leonidas expeditionary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Marines to get new drone-killing microwave weapon...23 Sept 2024 — The high-power microwave weapon prototype, known as Leoni...</span></span></span>

This variant emerged from collaboration involving the Office of Naval Research, Marine Corps Warfighting Laboratory and Joint Counter-small Unmanned Aircraft Systems Office. Development focused on countering drone [swarms]({{ 'swarms/' | relative_url }}) in austere environments where logistics, transportability and rapid [deployment]({{ 'deployment/' | relative_url }}) matter as much as raw performance. The programme was launched under a $5.5 million contract and specifically targeted expeditionary short-range air-defence requirements. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://defensescoop.com/2024/09/23/marines-microwave-weapon-hpm-epirus-leonidas-expeditionary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defensescoop.com">[DefenseScoop+2Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defensescoop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">marines microwave weapon hpm epirus leonidas expeditionary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Marines to get new drone-killing microwave weapon...23 Sept 2024 — The high-power microwave weapon prototype, known as Leoni...</span></span></span>

A notable aspect of Expeditionary Leonidas is that it represents adaptation rather than replacement. The same underlying microwave technology is retained, but the operational assumptions change. Marines operating from temporary island positions, dispersed coastal sites or forward expeditionary bases need systems that can be moved quickly and established with minimal infrastructure. ExDECS was designed around those realities. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://militaryembedded.com/unmanned/counter-uas/directed-energy-counter-swarm-system-under-development-for-us-marine-corps" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: militaryembedded.com">[Military Embedded Systems]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">militaryembedded.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Featured Companies</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Military Embedded SystemsDirected energy counter-swarm system under development...Epirus is scheduled to deliver a completed ExDECS HPM...</span></span></span>

By 2025, Epirus had delivered an ExDECS prototype for Marine Corps testing after government acceptance evaluations, moving the concept from development into operational experimentation. The transition illustrated how Leonidas was evolving into a family of mission-specific products rather than a single Army-focused programme. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-delivers-exdecs-hpm-prototype-to-u-s-navy-for-marine-corps-counter-drone-swarm-capability" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2The Defense Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy for...29 Apr 2025 — Leonidas Expeditionary was introduced in 2024 and is the latest addition to the company&#x27;s suite of scalable hig...</span></span></span>

## Maritime and Autonomous Variants

### Leonidas H2O Takes the Concept to Sea

The maritime variant, Leonidas H2O, represents perhaps the clearest example of the company's attempt to broaden the market beyond airborne drones. Introduced in 2025, the system applies the same high-power microwave principle to maritime security challenges, including unmanned surface vessels, small boats and aerial drones operating around naval forces. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Press Release</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — Epirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave System fo...</span></span></span>

What makes H2O notable is that it shifts the target set. Instead of focusing exclusively on flying drones, testing explored the disruption of commercially available outboard boat motors during US Navy exercises. This demonstrated that the microwave approach could potentially affect electronic systems associated with maritime threats as well as aerial ones.
