## Introduction

A laser defence system does not need to know merely that something is in the sky. It needs a fire-control track: a continuously updated estimate of a target’s position, speed, direction and future location that is accurate enough to keep a laser beam on a small point for seconds at a time. That requirement is far more demanding than simple detection. A radar contact may indicate that an object exists, but a successful laser engagement depends on combining radar, electro-optical (EO), infrared (IR) and sometimes radio-frequency (RF) observations into a single, high-confidence track. The process is known as sensor fusion, and it is one of the most important enabling technologies behind modern directed-energy weapons. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thalesgroup.com/en/solutions-catalogue/defence/naval/naval-fire-control-sensors" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thalesgroup.com">[Thales Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thalesgroup.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Thales GroupNaval Fire Control SensorsRadar, infrared and electro-optical fire control sensors providing crucial data for target acquisit...</span></span></span>

The challenge is that every sensor sees the world differently. Radar excels at wide-area search, EO cameras provide visual detail, infrared sensors reveal heat signatures, and RF systems may detect control links or emissions. Individually, each sensor has blind spots. Together, they can create the precision track required for [laser fire]({{ 'a-laser-should-fire-before-a-missile/' | relative_url }}) control. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beyondsky.xyz/blog/hardware/how-counter-drone-systems-work-radar-rf-ai-sensor-fusion" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beyondsky.xyz">[Beyond SKY+2NLR Reports]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beyondsky.xyz</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Beyond SKYHow Counter-Drone systems work: Radar, RF, AI &amp; Sensor</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 11, 2026 — Discover how modern counter-drone systems use radar, RF detection, AI, and sensor fusion to detect, track, and neutraliz...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 11, 2026</span></span></span>

## From Wide-Area Radar Contact to Confirmed Target

Most laser defence engagements begin with a sensor that can search large volumes of airspace. In practice, this is often a surveillance radar. Radar can scan continuously and detect moving objects at long range, providing the first indication that a potential threat exists. However, the initial radar track usually contains uncertainty. The object may be a drone, a bird, atmospheric clutter or another aircraft. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thalesgroup.com/en/solutions-catalogue/defence/naval/naval-fire-control-sensors" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thalesgroup.com">[Thales Group+2Clear Align]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thalesgroup.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Thales GroupNaval Fire Control SensorsRadar, infrared and electro-optical fire control sensors providing crucial data for target acquisit...</span></span></span>

Sensor fusion begins by treating that first detection as a cue rather than a final answer. Command-and-control software distributes the radar track to other sensors, directing them toward a specific area of sky. Instead of searching the entire horizon, EO and IR sensors are pointed toward the predicted location of the contact. This dramatically reduces the search problem. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.antidronesuav.com/what-is-eo-system-in-counter-drone/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: antidronesuav.com">[antidronesuav]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">antidronesuav.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EO system in counter drone?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EO system in counter drone? - NexSkyFebruary 4, 2026 — EO/IR system counter-drone explained: what it is, how it spot...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2026</span></span></span>

The software must then decide whether observations from different sensors refer to the same object. If a radar track and an infrared hotspot move in a compatible way, the system associates them into a single target record. Rather than maintaining separate radar and camera tracks, the fire-control system creates one fused track with a confidence score and continuously updated state estimate. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/US11199379B1/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patents.google.com">[Google Patents]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patents.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google PatentsEOIR and RF sensors fusion and tracking using a dual...The system and method for EO/IR and RF sensor fusion and tracking u...</span></span></span>

This step is critical because laser weapons generally operate against small targets where tracking uncertainty directly affects beam effectiveness. ## How Optical and Infrared Sensors Refine the Track

Once the target has been cued, EO and IR sensors usually become the primary source of precision tracking. Radar may indicate where the object is, but cameras often provide far greater angular accuracy and allow the system to lock onto specific visual or thermal features. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://clearalign.com/knowledge-center/id/30/why-eoir-systems-are-surpassing-radar-in-the-modern-battlespace" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: clearalign.com">[Clear Align+2Institute for Defense Analyses]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">clearalign.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Clear AlignWhy EO/IR Systems Are Surpassing Radar in the Modern...Unlike radar, which can struggle to detect small, low-flying drones, E...</span></span></span>

An EO camera can distinguish shape, movement patterns and structural details. An infrared sensor can continue tracking when lighting conditions are poor and can often detect heat signatures that are difficult to see in visible light. By combining both streams, the system gains a more robust picture than either sensor could provide alone. Saab's CEROS fire-control director, for example, incorporates simultaneous TV and IR tracking as part of its sensor-fusion process, allowing correlation of multiple sensor views into a more reliable track. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.saab.com/contentassets/669f85167100453889741aff1fb6cd2d/ceros-200.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saab.com">[Start]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Start Fire control director</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">StartFire control directorDecember 13, 2019 — It includes sensor fusion, i.e. simultaneous TV and IR tracking, correlation tracking and b...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 13, 2019</span></span></span>

At this stage, tracking algorithms become as important as the sensors themselves. The software predicts where the target should appear in the next instant, compares that prediction with new measurements, and updates the track. Many systems use [variants]({{ 'variants/' | relative_url }}) of Kalman filtering or related estimation methods to merge measurements with differing accuracy, update rates and noise characteristics. Patented EO/IR and RF fusion systems explicitly describe adaptive filtering approaches that dynamically weight sensor inputs according to geometry and confidence. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/US11199379B1/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patents.google.com">[Google Patents]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patents.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google PatentsEOIR and RF sensors fusion and tracking using a dual...The system and method for EO/IR and RF sensor fusion and tracking u...</span></span></span>

The result is not a simple average of sensor readings. Instead, the system continuously asks:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* Which sensor currently has the most reliable view?
* Which measurement is likely corrupted by clutter or noise?
* Are all sensors observing the same object?
* How certain is the current track prediction?

</div>

The answer changes moment by moment. A radar track may dominate during long-range detection, while EO/IR measurements become dominant as the target approaches engagement range. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.chess-dynamics.com/capabilities-fire-control" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: chess-dynamics.com">[Chess Dynamics]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">chess-dynamics.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Chess DynamicsFire ControlThis fusion of radar and EO data enhances overall tracking accuracy and reliability. For laser systems, this refined track is eventually passed to a beam director and fine-tracking subsystem. These components make extremely small pointing corrections while maintaining alignment between the laser beam and the target. ## Why Sensor Handoff Failures Can Lose the Shot

One of the least visible but most important stages in sensor fusion is the handoff between sensors. A target may first appear on radar, then be acquired by an EO/IR tracker, and finally be transferred to a fine-tracking system linked directly to the laser beam director. Every transfer introduces risk. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.antidronesuav.com/what-is-eo-system-in-counter-drone/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: antidronesuav.com">[antidronesuav]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">antidronesuav.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EO system in counter drone?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EO system in counter drone? - NexSkyFebruary 4, 2026 — EO/IR system counter-drone explained: what it is, how it spot...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2026</span></span></span>

* The camera cannot find the target inside the radar uncertainty region.
* Multiple objects appear in the same field of view and the wrong one is selected.
* Atmospheric effects degrade the optical image.
* Latency causes the camera to point at where the target was rather than where it is.
* The target manoeuvres during the transfer process. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.antidronesuav.com/what-is-eo-system-in-counter-drone/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: antidronesuav.com">[antidronesuav+2Cedrat technologies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">antidronesuav.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EO system in counter drone?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EO system in counter drone? - NexSkyFebruary 4, 2026 — EO/IR system counter-drone explained: what it is, how it spot...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2026</span></span></span>

If that cueing process is delayed or inaccurate, the camera may never acquire the target in time for engagement. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.antidronesuav.com/what-is-eo-system-in-counter-drone/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: antidronesuav.com">[antidronesuav]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">antidronesuav.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EO system in counter drone?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EO system in counter drone? - NexSkyFebruary 4, 2026 — EO/IR system counter-drone explained: what it is, how it spot...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2026</span></span></span>

## The Real Product of Sensor Fusion: Confidence

The most valuable output of sensor fusion is not a radar plot, a thermal image or a camera lock. It is confidence. A laser weapon must know where the target is now, where it will be moments later, and how certain the system is about both estimates. Sensor fusion transforms separate observations into that confidence-weighted fire-control track. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/US11199379B1/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patents.google.com">[Google Patents]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patents.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google PatentsEOIR and RF sensors fusion and tracking using a dual...The system and method for EO/IR and RF sensor fusion and tracking u...</span></span></span>
