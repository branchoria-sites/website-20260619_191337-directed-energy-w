## Introduction

High-power microwave (HPM) air-defence systems are designed to disable electronics rather than strike a single physical point. That distinction explains why friendly systems can be affected alongside hostile drones. A microwave pulse interacts with electrical conductors, antennas, wiring and electronic circuits wherever they are exposed to sufficient electromagnetic energy. The beam does not identify ownership, mission or intent; it only encounters electronic pathways capable of receiving and conducting energy. As a result, a [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) microwave engagement can create a zone in which nearby friendly radios, sensors, drones or other electronic equipment may experience disruption, degraded performance or, in extreme cases, damage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

## How Radio-Frequency Energy Couples Into Electronics

Microwave weapons work because electronic systems contain many structures that unintentionally behave like receivers. Antennas are the most obvious example, but they are far from the only pathway.

When intense microwave energy encounters a piece of equipment, conductive elements can collect and channel that energy into sensitive electronics. Potential entry routes include:

Engineers often distinguish between “front-door” and “back-door” coupling. Front-door coupling occurs when energy enters through a system's intended receiving path, such as a radio or radar antenna. Back-door coupling occurs when energy penetrates through cables, power lines, enclosure openings or other unintended routes. Both mechanisms can inject voltages and currents into circuits that were designed for far weaker signals.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/3056672_Susceptibility_of_Electronic_Systems_to_High-Power_Microwaves_Summary_of_Test_Experience" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate+2bnbasu.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Research Gate Susceptibility of Electronic Systems to High-Power ...A substantial body of experimental studies has demonstrated that HPM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGateSusceptibility of Electronic Systems to High-Power ...A substantial body of experimental studies has demonstrated that HPM ca...</span></span></span>

Once inside the equipment, the induced electrical effects can range from minor to severe. Circuits may experience temporary logic errors, software crashes, resets, communication failures, latch-up conditions, degraded performance or permanent semiconductor damage if electrical thresholds are exceeded. The exact outcome depends on frequency, pulse characteristics, equipment design and the amount of energy successfully coupled into the system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.everythingrf.com/community/high-power-microwave-hpm-technology-in-modern-defense-applications-capabilities-challenges" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: everythingrf.com">[everythingrf.com+2OSTI.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">everythingrf.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">When exposed to high-field-strength...Read more...</span></span></span>

For friendly forces, this means that a radio network, surveillance sensor or autonomous vehicle does not need to be the intended target to experience consequences. It only needs to present a sufficiently efficient coupling path while located within the relevant electromagnetic environment.

## Why Area Effects Differ From Laser Aim Points

The friendly-electronics problem is fundamentally tied to the way microwave effects are delivered.

A laser typically concentrates energy on a specific surface location. Although [lasers]({{ 'lasers/' | relative_url }}) have their own [collateral]({{ 'collateral-risk/' | relative_url }})-effect considerations, the damaging mechanism is generally associated with the illuminated spot. Microwave systems instead seek to place electromagnetic energy into electronics across a volume of space. Their military value often comes from affecting multiple targets simultaneously, especially drone swarms.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onr.navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onr.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

This creates an important operational difference. A laser engagement is usually framed as a question of whether the beam strikes a particular object. A microwave engagement is better understood as a question of which electronic systems occupy the affected electromagnetic volume and how vulnerable they are to coupling effects. The US Government Accountability Office has specifically noted that wider-beam directed-energy systems can affect assets in an area “whether friend or foe”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

The effect volume is not necessarily uniform. Some locations within the beam may receive much stronger field strengths than others. [Reflections]({{ 'reflections/' | relative_url }}) from terrain, buildings, vehicles or structures can also alter local conditions. Consequently, two similar friendly devices separated by a relatively short distance may experience very different outcomes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://user.eng.umd.edu/~vlg/MSIC%20lectures.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: user.eng.umd.edu">[user.eng.umd.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">user.eng.umd.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">High Power Microwave Technology and Effects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Microwave Coupling Presenter: Mechanisms in Electronic Devices. This is why microwave air defence is often discussed as a counter-electronics capability rather than a precision point-target weapon. The operational question is not merely where the beam is aimed, but which electronics exist within the broader region where meaningful electromagnetic coupling can occur.

## What Makes Outcomes Hard to Predict

One of the most challenging aspects of microwave effects is that susceptibility varies dramatically between systems.

Two radios built for the same purpose may respond differently because of variations in shielding, cable routing, enclosure design, grounding arrangements or component selection. ## Why Friendly Systems Can Resemble Hostile Targets

Many modern drones, radios and sensor systems rely on commercially available electronic components and broadly similar architectures. From the perspective of an intense microwave field, a hostile drone flight controller and a friendly drone flight controller may not look very different.

Microwave energy interacts with conductors, semiconductors and circuit structures. It does not recognise operational categories. If a friendly unmanned aircraft, communications relay or sensor package presents a vulnerable coupling path, it can experience the same classes of upset mechanisms that make microwave weapons effective against hostile drones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onr.navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research+2everythingrf.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onr.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

## The Core Mechanism Behind Friendly-Electronics Risk

The friendly-electronics risk from microwave air defence does not arise because microwave weapons are inaccurate in the conventional sense. It arises because their intended mechanism is the interaction of electromagnetic energy with electronics. Any nearby system that provides a path for that energy to enter sensitive circuitry can potentially experience effects.

In practical terms, microwave weapons act on a population of electronic devices within an effect volume rather than on a single physical point. Their ability to engage multiple drones simultaneously comes from that broad electronic reach. The same reach is also what creates uncertainty for friendly radios, sensors, autonomous systems and other electronics operating nearby.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106717" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[GAO+2Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">gao 23 106717</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science &amp; Tech Spotlight: Directed Energy Weapons25 May 2023 — For example, wider beam DEWs, such as high power microwave or millimete...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>
