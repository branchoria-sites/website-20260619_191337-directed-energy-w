## Introduction

A laser does not have to shoot a drone down to stop it being effective. In many cases, it only needs to prevent the drone from seeing. Modern unmanned aircraft depend heavily on electro-optical and infrared cameras for navigation, reconnaissance, target identification and operator control. A laser dazzler exploits that dependence by directing intense light into the drone’s imaging system, overwhelming the sensor and making the video feed partially or completely unusable. This is the principle behind the optical-dazzler element incorporated into systems such as HELIOS: attack the information source rather than the airframe itself. When the camera can no longer provide reliable imagery, the drone may remain airborne but fail in its mission. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/us-navys-answer-sea-launched-drones-lasers-199479" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalinterest.org">[The National Interest]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalinterest.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Interest The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy&#x27;s Answer To Sea-launched Drones: LasersJanuary 19, 2022 — “The laser is meant to zap electro-optical sensors, like digital video cam...</span></span></span>

## What Dazzling Does to Camera Feeds

At the heart of laser dazzling is a simple problem for imaging sensors: they can process only a limited amount of incoming light. When a laser beam enters a camera lens, it concentrates a large amount of optical energy onto a small area of the detector. The affected pixels become saturated and can no longer record useful image information. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Visible-and-near-infrared-laser-dazzling-of-CCD-and-Santos-Chr%C3%A9tien/b776c9591df417b3fbedb24995e917e0efff0160" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: semanticscholar.org">[Semantic Scholar+2ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">semanticscholar.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on semanticscholar.org.</span></span></span>

The visible result depends on the [laser power]({{ 'power-costs/' | relative_url }}), [wavelength]({{ 'wavelengths/' | relative_url }}) and camera design, but common effects include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Bright white or coloured blooms that obscure part of the image.
* Large overexposed regions spreading across the frame.
* Loss of contrast and detail around the illuminated area.
* Temporary washout of the entire scene.
* In more severe cases, permanent degradation of detector elements. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Visible-and-near-infrared-laser-dazzling-of-CCD-and-Santos-Chr%C3%A9tien/b776c9591df417b3fbedb24995e917e0efff0160" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: semanticscholar.org">[Semantic Scholar+2ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">semanticscholar.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on semanticscholar.org.</span></span></span>

</div>

Researchers studying CCD and CMOS cameras have shown that increasing laser intensity enlarges the saturated area seen by the sensor. The result is not merely a bright spot; optical scattering inside the lens and sensor system can spread the effect across a much larger portion of the image than the laser beam itself occupies. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279953633_Laser_dazzling_of_focal_plane_array_cameras" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">In this paper we will discuss the results of...Read more...</span></span></span>

A useful analogy is looking directly into a bright spotlight at night. The light source occupies only a small part of the field of view, yet glare can prevent the observer from seeing much of the surrounding scene. Laser dazzling applies the same principle with far greater intensity and precision. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Visible-and-near-infrared-laser-dazzling-of-CCD-and-Santos-Chr%C3%A9tien/b776c9591df417b3fbedb24995e917e0efff0160" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: semanticscholar.org">[Semantic Scholar]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">semanticscholar.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on semanticscholar.org.</span></span></span>

### Why Lasers Are Especially Effective Against Cameras

Ordinary illumination spreads over a scene. A laser concentrates energy into a narrow, coherent beam that can be directed precisely at the camera aperture. Because camera optics are designed to collect and focus light efficiently, the lens effectively helps deliver the dazzling energy onto the detector. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://liu.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1591091/FULLTEXT01.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: liu.diva-portal.org">[liu.diva-portal.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">liu.diva-portal.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The retroreflection from the target is used as a feedback signal. ## Why Small Drones Depend So Heavily on Optical Payloads

The effectiveness of dazzling stems from the central role that cameras play in modern unmanned systems. Many drones carry visible-light cameras, thermal imagers or combined electro-optical/infrared payloads for surveillance, reconnaissance and targeting. Operators often rely on live video as their primary source of situational awareness. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unmannedsystemstechnology.com/expo/electro-optical-systems/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unmannedsystemstechnology.com">[Unmanned Systems Technology+2ukrspecsystems.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unmannedsystemstechnology.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">electro optical systems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unmanned Systems TechnologyElectro-Optical Systems for Drones &amp; UAV25 Sept 2025 — Find Electro-Optical Camera &amp; Gimbal manufacturers &amp; EO...</span></span></span>

For small and medium-sized drones, optical sensors commonly support:

* First-person-view piloting.
* Target detection and identification.
* Intelligence gathering.
* Navigation in complex environments.
* Battle damage assessment and surveillance. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unmannedsystemstechnology.com/expo/electro-optical-systems/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unmannedsystemstechnology.com">[Unmanned Systems Technology+2Drone Warfare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unmannedsystemstechnology.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">electro optical systems</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unmanned Systems TechnologyElectro-Optical Systems for Drones &amp; UAV25 Sept 2025 — Find Electro-Optical Camera &amp; Gimbal manufacturers &amp; EO...</span></span></span>

## When Blindness Is Enough Without a Kill

The most important feature of a dazzler is that it can achieve military effects without destroying the aircraft. If a reconnaissance drone loses the ability to observe, record or identify targets, the mission may fail even though the drone remains physically intact. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/us-navys-answer-sea-launched-drones-lasers-199479" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalinterest.org">[The National Interest]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalinterest.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The National Interest The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy&#x27;s Answer To Sea-launched Drones: LasersJanuary 19, 2022 — “The laser is meant to zap electro-optical sensors, like digital video cam...</span></span></span>

Several operational outcomes can follow:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* A remote operator loses visual references needed to continue the mission.
* Surveillance imagery becomes unusable.
* Target tracking and identification degrade.
* Autonomous vision-based functions become unreliable.

### Temporary Interference Versus Permanent Damage

Not every dazzling event destroys a camera. The outcome depends on exposure time, wavelength, sensor characteristics and delivered energy. At lower levels, the effect may be temporary glare or [saturation]({{ 'saturation/' | relative_url }}). ## Why Sensor Dazzling Fits the HELIOS Concept

The optical-dazzler component of HELIOS reflects a broader shift in directed-energy thinking. Modern military systems increasingly depend on sensors for detection, navigation, targeting and intelligence collection. Disrupting those sensors can create operational effects disproportionate to the energy applied. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Energy_Laser_with_Integrated_Optical-dazzler_and_Surveillance" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzler and Surveillance</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzler and Surveillance</span></span></span>

In practical terms, a drone with a functioning airframe but a blinded camera may be no more useful than a destroyed drone. That is why sensor dazzling is often viewed as a distinct counter-drone mechanism rather than merely a lesser version of laser destruction.
