## Introduction

The most important aspect of HELIOS is not that it is a laser. It is that it is a laser connected to the same combat architecture that already manages a destroyer’s sensors, tracks, missiles, guns and command decisions. Earlier naval laser programmes proved that a beam could damage targets at sea. HELIOS aims to prove something more difficult: that a directed-energy weapon can function as a normal part of an operational warship’s combat system. That integration with the Aegis Combat System is what makes HELIOS a meaningful step towards practical naval directed-energy warfare rather than another technology demonstration. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2Maritime Executive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon SystemJan 9, 2021 — The High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzle...</span></span></span>

## Why Combat-System Integration Changes the Problem

Most discussions of naval [lasers]({{ 'lasers/' | relative_url }}) focus on beam power, range or weather limitations. Those factors matter, but they are only part of the challenge. A warship does not fight as a collection of separate devices. It fights through a combat system that fuses radar tracks, identifies threats, prioritises targets and assigns weapons.

Aegis already performs this role for missiles, guns and other defensive systems. By integrating HELIOS into that architecture, the Navy is attempting to make the laser another selectable weapon within the ship's broader defensive network rather than an isolated piece of equipment. Lockheed Martin has repeatedly described HELIOS as an integrated weapon system rather than simply a laser, emphasising its connection to ship systems and combat management functions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon SystemJan 9, 2021 — The High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzle...</span></span></span>

This explains why official descriptions of HELIOS consistently stress integration. The 2018 contract announcement described it as a fully integrated laser weapon system, while later Navy and industry statements highlighted its installation on an Aegis-equipped destroyer and its connection to existing combat functions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://news.lockheedmartin.com/2018-03-01-Lockheed-Martin-Receives-150-Million-Contract-to-Deliver-Integrated-High-Energy-Laser-Weapon-Systems-to-U-S-Navy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: news.lockheedmartin.com">[Media - Lockheed Martin+2Media - Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">news.lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Media - Lockheed MartinLockheed Martin Receives $150 Million Contract to Deliver...Mar 1, 2018 — Lockheed Martin Receives $150 Million C...</span></span></span>

The practical consequence is that HELIOS can potentially receive tracks already generated elsewhere in the ship. Instead of independently searching for targets, the laser can be cued by the destroyer's existing sensor network. This reduces reaction time and allows the weapon to participate in the same engagement process used by other defensive systems. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/aegis-combat-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2Maritime Executive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinAegis Combat SystemAEGIS Weapon System is a centralized, automated, command-and-control (C2) and weapons control system de...</span></span></span>

## How Cueing and Deconfliction Shape Laser Use

A laser engagement begins long before a beam reaches a target. The crucial question is how the ship determines which threat should be engaged, when it should be engaged and which weapon should be used.

### Cueing the Laser

Modern destroyers generate enormous amounts of tracking data from radar, electro-optical sensors and other surveillance systems. Aegis continuously evaluates these tracks and assesses threats. Integration allows HELIOS to benefit from that existing picture rather than operating as a separate sensor-to-shooter chain. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/aegis-combat-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinAegis Combat SystemAEGIS Weapon System is a centralized, automated, command-and-control (C2) and weapons control system de...</span></span></span>

This is particularly important against small drones. Detecting a drone can be harder than damaging it. If the combat system already knows where the target is and can hand that information to the laser, engagement becomes faster and more reliable. The laser becomes one option within a larger sensor network rather than a standalone detector and shooter. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon SystemJan 9, 2021 — The High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzle...</span></span></span>

### Avoiding Conflicts with Other Weapons

Integration also helps solve deconfliction problems. A destroyer may have electronic-warfare systems, missiles, guns and a laser all available simultaneously. Without a central combat system, different systems could compete for the same target or interfere with one another.

Aegis exists precisely to manage such decisions. It can determine whether a threat should be jammed, dazzled, engaged by the laser, fired upon with a gun or intercepted with a missile. HELIOS therefore gains value not merely from what it can destroy, but from its ability to fit into an established command-and-control process. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/aegis-combat-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin+2Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinAegis Combat SystemAEGIS Weapon System is a centralized, automated, command-and-control (C2) and weapons control system de...</span></span></span>

### The Multi-Mission Advantage

HELIOS also complicates traditional weapon assignment because it is not only a destructive laser. The system combines surveillance functions, an optical dazzler and a high-energy laser in a single package. That means commanders can potentially use the same system for observation, sensor disruption or physical damage depending on circumstances. Integration allows those functions to contribute directly to the broader combat picture. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2021/more-than-a-laser-helios-is-an-integrated-weapon-system.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lockheedmartin.com">[Lockheed Martin]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lockheedmartin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">more than a laser helios is an integrated weapon system</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lockheed MartinMore Than a Laser, HELIOS is an Integrated Weapon SystemJan 9, 2021 — The High Energy Laser with Integrated Optical-dazzle...</span></span></span>

## What Commanders Gain from Another Response Option

The operational value of HELIOS is best understood as expanding a commander’s menu of choices.

Traditionally, a destroyer facing a small drone might need to decide between ignoring it, attempting electronic warfare, firing a gun or expending a missile.
