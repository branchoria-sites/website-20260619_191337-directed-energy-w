## Introduction

A microwave [counter-drone]({{ 'missile-savings/' | relative_url }}) weapon does not need to destroy every drone individually to be valuable. Its main advantage is that a single pulse can affect multiple aircraft occupying the same volume of airspace, reducing swarm size in batches rather than forcing a defender into a sequence of one-on-one engagements. In swarm defence, this matters because the limiting factor is often time. When dozens of drones arrive together, a weapon that can simultaneously disrupt electronics across part of the formation can rapidly reduce the number of active threats and buy precious seconds for follow-on defences. High-power microwave (HPM) and radio-frequency directed energy systems are therefore designed around a one-to-many engagement model rather than the serial engagement model typical of most conventional interceptors and [lasers]({{ 'lasers/' | relative_url }}). <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves HPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

## Why Swarm Attacks Compress Defensive Timing

Drone [swarms]({{ 'swarms/' | relative_url }}) create a throughput problem. Even if a defensive system can reliably defeat a single drone, success against a swarm depends on how many targets can be engaged before the formation reaches its objective.

This is where microwave effects become attractive. A microwave pulse propagates at the speed of light and can illuminate a broader region than a tightly focused laser beam. Instead of selecting one drone, dwelling on it, and then moving to the next, the weapon attempts to inject disruptive electromagnetic energy into multiple drones' electronics at once. If several drones are exposed within the effective beam volume, several may be affected during the same engagement window. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research+2RTX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves HPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

The practical consequence is not merely faster destruction. It is a different engagement geometry. A defender may be able to reduce a 40-drone threat to a much smaller and less coordinated group after only a handful of pulses, avoiding the queue problem that swarms are designed to create.

## How Wide-Area Pulses Create Batch Effects

The mechanism behind batch defeat is electronic coupling rather than physical impact.

High-power microwave systems generate intense electromagnetic energy intended to interact with electronic circuits. Depending on the design of both the weapon and the drone, the pulse may overload components, upset processors, corrupt signals, disrupt sensors, or damage electronics outright. The objective is to make the drone unable to complete its mission, whether through permanent failure or temporary disruption. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research+2RTX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves HPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

Batch effects emerge because the pulse is not restricted to a single point target. If multiple drones occupy the illuminated area:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Several drones may suffer complete electronic failure.
* Others may lose navigation or flight-control functions.
* Some may experience communications interruptions.
* Others may enter failsafe modes or lose swarm coordination.

</div>

Not every aircraft must be destroyed for the pulse to be operationally useful. The important outcome is that a significant fraction of the swarm becomes ineffective simultaneously. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves HPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

The broad-beam nature of many HPM concepts explains why developers consistently market them as counter-swarm systems. Their value comes from affecting a volume of airspace rather than surgically attacking one airframe at a time. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research+2Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves HPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

## What Real Demonstrations Reveal About Batch Defeat

Recent demonstrations have increasingly focused on proving one-to-many engagement capability rather than merely showing that a microwave weapon can disable a drone.

In April 2025, the British Ministry of Defence announced successful trials of a radio-frequency directed energy weapon that defeated drone swarms. According to official reporting, soldiers brought down two separate swarms during a single engagement sequence, while more than 100 drones were immobilised across testing. ## What Partial Disruption Can Still Accomplish

A common misunderstanding is that microwave weapons are only useful if they permanently destroy electronics. In practice, partial disruption can produce significant operational effects.

Modern swarms often depend on coordination. Drones may share navigation information, exchange commands, maintain formation spacing, or synchronise attacks. If a microwave pulse simultaneously disrupts a portion of those systems, the swarm can lose coherence even when some aircraft remain airborne. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.onr.navy.mil/organization/departments/code-35/division-353/directed-energy-weapons-high-power-microwaves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: navy.mil">[Office of Naval Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of Naval Research Directed Energy Weapons: High Power Microwaves HPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Naval ResearchDirected Energy Weapons: High Power MicrowavesHPM weapons create beams of electromagnetic energy over a broad spe...</span></span></span>

Several outcomes can benefit the defender:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* The swarm may fragment into smaller groups.
* Individual drones may drift off course.
* Collision risk within the formation may increase.
* Some aircraft may revert to emergency behaviours.
* Surviving drones may become easier targets for other defences.

</div>

From a defensive perspective, reducing a coordinated swarm of forty drones to ten functioning aircraft can be almost as valuable as destroying all forty if it restores manageable engagement rates.

This is why official descriptions of HPM effects frequently refer to disruption as well as destruction. ## Why Batch Defeat Matters More Than Perfect Defeat

The key contribution of microwave weapons to swarm defence is not necessarily greater lethality. It is higher engagement throughput.
