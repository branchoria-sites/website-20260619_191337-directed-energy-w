## Introduction

Leonidas H2O represents a significant shift in the Leonidas family of directed-energy weapons. Earlier Leonidas systems were primarily associated with defeating drone [swarms]({{ 'swarms/' | relative_url }}) by disrupting their electronics with high-power microwave (HPM) energy. Leonidas H2O extends that same counter-electronics concept into the maritime domain, targeting small boats, unmanned surface vessels (USVs) and aerial drones with a common microwave effect. The key development is not simply that the system can operate near water; it is that it demonstrated the ability to disable outboard boat motors during US Navy testing, addressing a long-standing requirement for a non-kinetic vessel-stopping capability.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Naval News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — The system is built using the scalable Leonidas energy-based...</span></span></span>

## Why Maritime Threats Changed the Target Set

Naval and coastal forces increasingly face threats that blur the line between aircraft and vessels. Small unmanned surface vessels, remotely operated explosive boats, autonomous reconnaissance craft and low-cost fast attack boats all rely heavily on electronic control systems. At the same time, many maritime operations must contend with aerial drones operating above the water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://defensescoop.com/2024/04/04/epirus-navy-hpm-microwave-antx-coastal-trident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defensescoop.com">[DefenseScoop]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defensescoop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">epirus navy hpm microwave antx coastal trident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy to test Epirus high-power microwave&#x27;s ability to zap...4 Apr 2024 — The Navy is getting ready to test the ability of Ep...</span></span></span>

This environment created a natural opportunity for high-power microwave technology. The same electromagnetic effects that can disable drone electronics can also interfere with the control and power-management systems used by modern boat engines and autonomous vessels. Rather than developing an entirely new weapon, Epirus adapted its existing Leonidas architecture into a maritime-focused configuration known as Leonidas H2O.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Defence Industry]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — The system is built using the scalable Leonidas energy-based...</span></span></span>

## What Outboard-Motor Disruption Showed

The most notable evidence behind Leonidas H2O comes from testing conducted through the US Navy's Advanced Naval Technology Exercise Coastal Trident (ANTX-CT) programme. During those demonstrations, the system was evaluated against commercially available outboard motors ranging from 40 to 90 horsepower. According to Epirus and subsequent reporting, the prototype successfully affected all tested motors at operationally relevant distances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Naval News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — The system is built using the scalable Leonidas energy-based...</span></span></span>

The significance of these tests lies in what they reveal about the mechanism of microwave interdiction. Traditional vessel-stopping methods generally rely on:

* Explosive effects.

Leonidas H2O instead attempts to attack the electronic dependencies of the vessel. Modern outboard engines contain electronic control units, ignition management systems and digital components that can be vulnerable to intense electromagnetic energy. If those systems are disrupted, the engine can cease functioning even when the vessel itself remains physically intact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Defence Industry]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — The system is built using the scalable Leonidas energy-based...</span></span></span>

The tests also demonstrated effectiveness despite operating under constraints. Epirus stated that demonstrations occurred at roughly half of the system's potential output power and with limited waveform options, yet the system still achieved successful results against every target motor presented.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus+2Naval News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — The system is built using the scalable Leonidas energy-based...</span></span></span>

A microwave-based effect potentially offers a means of neutralising propulsion and control systems without the [collateral]({{ 'collateral-risk/' | relative_url }}) damage associated with missiles or naval gunfire.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-introduces-leonidas-h2o-energy-based-high-power-microwave-system-for-maritime-interdiction-and-uav-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epirusinc.com">[Epirus]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epirusinc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EpirusEpirus Introduces Leonidas H₂O, High-Power Microwave...8 Apr 2025 — The system is built using the scalable Leonidas energy-based...</span></span></span>

## Why Water Creates New Opportunities for Microwave Defence

The maritime environment is not simply another location for a land-based system. Water changes how sensors, communications and electromagnetic energy behave.

Reporting on Leonidas H2O has highlighted that maritime testing explored engagement geometries unique to the sea environment. Because the water surface can influence electromagnetic propagation, operators may be able to exploit [reflections]({{ 'reflections/' | relative_url }}) and other environmental effects that do not exist in the same way on land. Some observers have noted that water-surface reflections may help extend useful engagement conditions against certain targets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://industrievandaag.nl/microgolfwapens-leonidas-h2o/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: industrievandaag.nl">[Industrievandaag]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">industrievandaag.nl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Microgolfwapens stoppen drones en boten in één stoot</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Microgolfwapens stoppen drones en boten in één stootApril 11, 2025 — 11 Apr 2025 — Leonidas H2O: hoe microgolfwapens dron...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 11, 2025</span></span></span>

Equally important is the concentration of electronic systems aboard modern small craft. Autonomous vessels, remotely piloted boats and electronically controlled engines present numerous potential points of vulnerability.
